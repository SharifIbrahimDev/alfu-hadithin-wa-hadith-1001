### Hadith #0752 | الحديث رقم: 0752

#### 🇸🇦 القسم العربي (Arabic Text & Takhrij)
<div dir="rtl" align="right">

##### **[الباب: كُنْ فِي الدُّنْيَا كَأَنَّكَ غَرِيبٌ أَوْ عَابِرُ سَبِيلٍ]**

> **نَصُّ الْحَدِيثِ بِالتَّشْكِيلِ:**  
> عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُمَرَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: أَخَذَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ بِمَنْكِبِي فَقَالَ: **«كُنْ فِي الدُّنْيَا كَأَنَّكَ غَرِيبٌ أَوْ عَابِرُ سَبِيلٍ»**، وَكَانَ ابْنُ عُمَرَ يَقُولُ: **«إِذَا أَمْسَيْتَ فَلَا تَنْتَظِرِ الصَّبَاحَ، وَإِذَا أَصْبَحْتَ فَلَا تَنْتَظِرِ الْمَسَاءَ، وَخُذْ مِنْ صِحَّتِكَ لِمَرَضِكَ، وَمِنْ حَيَاتِكَ لِمَوْتِكَ»**.

* **الرَّاوِي:** عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ عُمَرَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا.
* **التَّخْرِيجُ:** صحيح البخاري (رقم 6416) — المكتبة الشاملة.
* **الْحُكْمُ:** صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)

</div>

#### 🇬🇧 English Translation & Commentary
##### **[Topic: Be in This World as a Stranger or a Wayfarer]**

> **English Translation:**  
> *"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ took hold of my shoulder and said: "Be in this world as though you were a stranger or a traveler passing along a road." And Ibn 'Umar used to say: "When evening comes, do not wait for the morning; and when morning comes, do not wait for the evening. Take from your health for your illness, and from your life for your death."*

* **Companion Narrator:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn 'Umar (رضي الله عنهما).
* **Canonical Reference:** صحيح البخاري (رقم 6416) — المكتبة الشاملة.
* **Scholarly Grading:** **صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)**
* **Key Lessons & Takeaways:** The worldly life is a temporary transit lounge; the wise believer remains unattached to transient luxuries and prepares daily provisions for the eternal home.

### Hadith #0754 | الحديث رقم: 0754

#### 🇸🇦 القسم العربي (Arabic Text & Takhrij)
<div dir="rtl" align="right">

##### **[الباب: الدُّنْيَا فِي الْآخِرَةِ كَمِثْلِ مَا يَجْعَلُ أَحَدُكُمْ إِصْبَعَهُ فِي الْيَمِّ]**

> **نَصُّ الْحَدِيثِ بِالتَّشْكِيلِ:**  
> عَنِ الْمُسْتَوْرِدِ بْنِ شَدَّادٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«وَاللَّهِ مَا الدُّنْيَا فِي الْآخِرَةِ إِلَّا مِثْلُ مَا يَجْعَلُ أَحَدُكُمْ إِصْبَعَهُ هَذِهِ - وَأَشَارَ يَحْيَى بِالسَّبَّابَةِ - فِي الْيَمِّ، فَلْيَنْظُرْ بِمَ تَرْجِعُ؟»**.

* **الرَّاوِي:** الْمُسْتَوْرِدُ بْنُ شَدَّادٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ.
* **التَّخْرِيجُ:** صحيح مسلم (رقم 2858) — المكتبة الشاملة.
* **الْحُكْمُ:** صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)

</div>

#### 🇬🇧 English Translation & Commentary
##### **[Topic: The World Compared to the Hereafter is Like a Drop in the Ocean]**

> **English Translation:**  
> *"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "By Allah, the worldly life compared to the Hereafter is nothing but like one of you dipping his finger into the vast ocean—let him see what tiny moisture it brings back!"*

* **Companion Narrator:** Narrated by Al-Mustawrid ibn Shaddad (رضي الله عنه).
* **Canonical Reference:** صحيح مسلم (رقم 2858) — المكتبة الشاملة.
* **Scholarly Grading:** **صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)**
* **Key Lessons & Takeaways:** All earthly centuries, empires, and riches combined amount to no more than a wet droplet relative to the infinite, boundless ocean of eternity.

### Hadith #0755 | الحديث رقم: 0755

#### 🇸🇦 القسم العربي (Arabic Text & Takhrij)
<div dir="rtl" align="right">

##### **[الباب: لَوْ كَانَتِ الدُّنْيَا تَعْدِلُ عِنْدَ اللَّهِ جَنَاحَ بَعُوضَةٍ]**

> **نَصُّ الْحَدِيثِ بِالتَّشْكِيلِ:**  
> عَنْ سَهْلِ بْنِ سَعْدٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«لَوْ كَانَتِ الدُّنْيَا تَعْدِلُ عِنْدَ اللَّهِ جَنَاحَ بَعُوضَةٍ، مَا سَقَى كَافِرًا مِنْهَا شَرْبَةَ مَاءٍ»**.

* **الرَّاوِي:** سَهْلُ بْنُ سَعْدٍ السَّاعِدِيُّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ.
* **التَّخْرِيجُ:** جامع الترمذي (رقم 2320)، وسنن ابن ماجه (رقم 4110) — صحيح.
* **الْحُكْمُ:** صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)

</div>

#### 🇬🇧 English Translation & Commentary
##### **[Topic: The World is Not Worth the Wing of a Mosquito to Allah]**

> **English Translation:**  
> *"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "If the worldly life were equivalent in the sight of Allah to even the weight of a mosquito's wing, He would not have given a disbeliever a single sip of water from it."*

* **Companion Narrator:** Narrated by Sahl ibn Sa'd (رضي الله عنه).
* **Canonical Reference:** جامع الترمذي (رقم 2320)، وسنن ابن ماجه (رقم 4110) — صحيح.
* **Scholarly Grading:** **صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)**
* **Key Lessons & Takeaways:** Material wealth is not an indicator of divine favor; Allah grants worldly goods to those He loves and those He rejects, but reserves faith for those He loves.

### Hadith #0756 | الحديث رقم: 0756

#### 🇸🇦 القسم العربي (Arabic Text & Takhrij)
<div dir="rtl" align="right">

##### **[الباب: الدُّنْيَا أَهْوَنُ عَلَى اللَّهِ مِنْ هَذَا عَلَى أَهْلِهِ]**

> **نَصُّ الْحَدِيثِ بِالتَّشْكِيلِ:**  
> عَنْ جَابِرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا: أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ مَرَّ بِالسُّوقِ، وَالنَّاسُ كَنَفَتَيْهِ، فَمَرَّ بِجَدْيٍ أَسَكَّ مَيِّتٍ، فَتَنَاوَلَهُ فَأَخَذَ بِأُذُنِهِ، ثُمَّ قَالَ: **«أَيُّكُمْ يُحِبُّ أَنَّ هَذَا لَهُ بِدِرْهَمٍ؟»** فَقَالُوا: مَا نُحِبُّ أَنَّهُ لَنَا بِشَيْءٍ، وَمَا نَصْنَعُ بِهِ؟ قَالَ: **«أَتُحِبُّونَ أَنَّهُ لَكُمْ؟»** قَالُوا: وَاللَّهِ لَوْ كَانَ حَيًّا كَانَ عَيْبًا فِيهِ؛ لِأَنَّهُ أَسَكُّ، فَكَيْفَ وَهُوَ مَيِّتٌ؟ فَقَالَ: **«فَوَاللَّهِ لَلدُّنْيَا أَهْوَنُ عَلَى اللَّهِ مِنْ هَذَا عَلَيْكُمْ»**.

* **الرَّاوِي:** جَابِرُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا.
* **التَّخْرِيجُ:** صحيح مسلم (رقم 2957) — المكتبة الشاملة.
* **الْحُكْمُ:** صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)

</div>

#### 🇬🇧 English Translation & Commentary
##### **[Topic: The Dead Lamb in the Street: The World is More Insignificant]**

> **English Translation:**  
> *"The Prophet ﷺ passed through the marketplace with people surrounding him. He passed by a dead, defective earless kid (young goat). He took hold of its ear and asked: "Which of you would love to buy this for a single Dirham?" They said: "We would not want it for anything; what would we do with it?" He asked: "Would you like it if it were given to you for free?" They said: "By Allah, even if it were alive it would be defective because of its deformed ears, so how about now when it is dead carcass?!" The Prophet ﷺ said: "By Allah, the world is more insignificant in the sight of Allah than this carcass is to you!"*

* **Companion Narrator:** Narrated by Jabir ibn 'Abdullah (رضي الله عنهما).
* **Canonical Reference:** صحيح مسلم (رقم 2957) — المكتبة الشاملة.
* **Scholarly Grading:** **صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)**
* **Key Lessons & Takeaways:** Sensational physical object lesson demonstrating the utter worthlessness of worldly delusion compared to eternal spiritual realities.

### Hadith #0758 | الحديث رقم: 0758

#### 🇸🇦 القسم العربي (Arabic Text & Takhrij)
<div dir="rtl" align="right">

##### **[الباب: ازْهَدْ فِي الدُّنْيَا يُحِبَّكَ اللَّهُ]**

> **نَصُّ الْحَدِيثِ بِالتَّشْكِيلِ:**  
> عَنْ سَهْلِ بْنِ سَعْدٍ السَّاعِدِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: أَتَى النَّبِيَّ ﷺ رَجُلٌ فَقَالَ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، دُلَّنِي عَلَى عَمَلٍ إِذَا أَنَا عَمِلْتُهُ أَحَبَّنِيَ اللَّهُ وَأَحَبَّنِيَ النَّاسُ، فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«ازْهَدْ فِي الدُّنْيَا يُحِبَّكَ اللَّهُ، وَازْهَدْ فِيمَا فِي أَيْدِي النَّاسِ يُحِبَّكَ النَّاسُ»**.

* **الرَّاوِي:** سَهْلُ بْنُ سَعْدٍ السَّاعِدِيُّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ.
* **التَّخْرِيجُ:** سنن ابن ماجه (رقم 4102)، ومستدرك الحاكم (رقم 7873) — حديث حسن.
* **الْحُكْمُ:** صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)

</div>

#### 🇬🇧 English Translation & Commentary
##### **[Topic: Renouncing Worldly Craving Brings the Love of Allah and People]**

> **English Translation:**  
> *"A man came to the Prophet ﷺ and said: "O Messenger of Allah, direct me to an action which, if I perform it, Allah will love me and people will love me." The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Renounce attachment to the world (practise Zuhd), and Allah will love you; and renounce craving what is in the hands of people, and people will love you."*

* **Companion Narrator:** Narrated by Sahl ibn Sa'd as-Sa'idi (رضي الله عنه).
* **Canonical Reference:** سنن ابن ماجه (رقم 4102)، ومستدرك الحاكم (رقم 7873) — حديث حسن.
* **Scholarly Grading:** **صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)**
* **Key Lessons & Takeaways:** Detachment from worldly obsession earns divine love, while respecting people's possessions and avoiding greed secures communal respect and affection.

### Hadith #0759 | الحديث رقم: 0759

#### 🇸🇦 القسم العربي (Arabic Text & Takhrij)
<div dir="rtl" align="right">

##### **[الباب: مَالِي وَلِلدُّنْيَا، مَا أَنَا وَالدُّنْيَا إِلَّا كَرَاكِبٍ]**

* **التَّخْرِيجُ:** جامع الترمذي (رقم 2377)، وسنن ابن ماجه (رقم 4109) — حديث حسن صحيح.
* **الْحُكْمُ:** صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)

</div>

#### 🇬🇧 English Translation & Commentary
##### **[Topic: Parable of the Rider Resting Under the Shade of a Tree]**

> **English Translation:**  
> *"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ slept on a rough reed mat, and he got up with its marks imprinted on his side. We said: "O Messenger of Allah, if only you allowed us to prepare a soft mattress for you!" He said: "What do I have to do with this world? I am in this world like nothing but a rider who sought shade under a tree for a brief hour, and then departed and left it behind."*

* **Companion Narrator:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (رضي الله عنه).
* **Canonical Reference:** جامع الترمذي (رقم 2377)، وسنن ابن ماجه (رقم 4109) — حديث حسن صحيح.
* **Scholarly Grading:** **صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)**
* **Key Lessons & Takeaways:** The Prophet's absolute asceticism and contentment: viewing earthly life as a momentary pause under the shade of a tree during a long journey.

### Hadith #0760 | الحديث رقم: 0760

#### 🇸🇦 القسم العربي (Arabic Text & Takhrij)
<div dir="rtl" align="right">

#### 🇬🇧 English Translation & Commentary
##### **[Topic: The Insatiable Greed of the Son of Adam for Wealth]**

> **English Translation:**  
> *"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "If the son of Adam had a valley full of gold, he would desire to have two valleys; and nothing will ever fill his mouth/eyes except the dust (of the grave). And Allah accepts the repentance of the one who repents."*

* **Companion Narrator:** Narrated by Anas ibn Malik (رضي الله عنه).
* **Canonical Reference:** صحيح البخاري (رقم 6439)، وصحيح مسلم (رقم 1048) — متفق عليه.
