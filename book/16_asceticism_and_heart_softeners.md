# 16. كِتَابُ الزُّهْدِ وَالرَّقَائِقِ
# Chapter 16: The Book of Asceticism & Heart-Softening Traditions

### Hadith #0752
* **Topic / Chapter:** Be in This World as a Stranger or a Wayfarer / كُنْ فِي الدُّنْيَا كَأَنَّكَ غَرِيبٌ أَوْ عَابِرُ سَبِيلٍ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُمَرَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: أَخَذَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ بِمَنْكِبِي فَقَالَ: **«كُنْ فِي الدُّنْيَا كَأَنَّكَ غَرِيبٌ أَوْ عَابِرُ سَبِيلٍ»**، وَكَانَ ابْنُ عُمَرَ يَقُولُ: **«إِذَا أَمْسَيْتَ فَلَا تَنْتَظِرِ الصَّبَاحَ، وَإِذَا أَصْبَحْتَ فَلَا تَنْتَظِرِ الْمَسَاءَ، وَخُذْ مِنْ صِحَّتِكَ لِمَرَضِكَ، وَمِنْ حَيَاتِكَ لِمَوْتِكَ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn 'Umar (رضي الله عنهما).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ took hold of my shoulder and said: "Be in this world as though you were a stranger or a traveler passing along a road." And Ibn 'Umar used to say: "When evening comes, do not wait for the morning; and when morning comes, do not wait for the evening. Take from your health for your illness, and from your life for your death."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6416 (Kitab ar-Riqaq, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** The worldly life is a temporary transit lounge; the wise believer remains unattached to transient luxuries and prepares daily provisions for the eternal home.

### Hadith #0753
* **Topic / Chapter:** Two Great Blessings in Which Many are Defrauded: Health and Free Time / نِعْمَتَانِ مَغْبُونٌ فِيهِمَا كَثِيرٌ مِنَ النَّاسِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: قَالَ النَّبِيُّ ﷺ: **«نِعْمَتَانِ مَغْبُونٌ فِيهِمَا كَثِيرٌ مِنَ النَّاسِ: الصِّحَّةُ وَالْفَرَاغُ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn 'Abbas (رضي الله عنهما).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Prophet ﷺ said: "Two blessings are such that many people are defrauded (suffer great loss) concerning them: good health and free time."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6412 (Kitab ar-Riqaq, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Physical vitality and leisure time are precious, fleeting capital; squandering them in frivolous distractions without building righteous deeds brings bitter regret.

### Hadith #0754
* **Topic / Chapter:** The World Compared to the Hereafter is Like a Drop in the Ocean / الدُّنْيَا فِي الْآخِرَةِ كَمِثْلِ مَا يَجْعَلُ أَحَدُكُمْ إِصْبَعَهُ فِي الْيَمِّ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنِ الْمُسْتَوْرِدِ بْنِ شَدَّادٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«وَاللَّهِ مَا الدُّنْيَا فِي الْآخِرَةِ إِلَّا مِثْلُ مَا يَجْعَلُ أَحَدُكُمْ إِصْبَعَهُ هَذِهِ - وَأَشَارَ يَحْيَى بِالسَّبَّابَةِ - فِي الْيَمِّ، فَلْيَنْظُرْ بِمَ تَرْجِعُ؟»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Al-Mustawrid ibn Shaddad (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "By Allah, the worldly life compared to the Hereafter is nothing but like one of you dipping his finger into the vast ocean—let him see what tiny moisture it brings back!"
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 2858 (Kitab al-Jannah wa Sifat Na'imiha, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** All earthly centuries, empires, and riches combined amount to no more than a wet droplet relative to the infinite, boundless ocean of eternity.

### Hadith #0755
* **Topic / Chapter:** The World is Not Worth the Wing of a Mosquito to Allah / لَوْ كَانَتِ الدُّنْيَا تَعْدِلُ عِنْدَ اللَّهِ جَنَاحَ بَعُوضَةٍ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ سَهْلِ بْنِ سَعْدٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«لَوْ كَانَتِ الدُّنْيَا تَعْدِلُ عِنْدَ اللَّهِ جَنَاحَ بَعُوضَةٍ، مَا سَقَى كَافِرًا مِنْهَا شَرْبَةَ مَاءٍ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Sahl ibn Sa'd (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "If the worldly life were equivalent in the sight of Allah to even the weight of a mosquito's wing, He would not have given a disbeliever a single sip of water from it."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2320, Sunan Ibn Majah 4110 (Sahih according to at-Tirmidhi and al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Material wealth is not an indicator of divine favor; Allah grants worldly goods to those He loves and those He rejects, but reserves faith for those He loves.

### Hadith #0756
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Dead Lamb in the Street: The World is More Insignificant / الدُّنْيَا أَهْوَنُ عَلَى اللَّهِ مِنْ هَذَا عَلَى أَهْلِهِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ جَابِرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا: أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ مَرَّ بِالسُّوقِ، وَالنَّاسُ كَنَفَتَيْهِ، فَمَرَّ بِجَدْيٍ أَسَكَّ مَيِّتٍ، فَتَنَاوَلَهُ فَأَخَذَ بِأُذُنِهِ، ثُمَّ قَالَ: **«أَيُّكُمْ يُحِبُّ أَنَّ هَذَا لَهُ بِدِرْهَمٍ؟»** فَقَالُوا: مَا نُحِبُّ أَنَّهُ لَنَا بِشَيْءٍ، وَمَا نَصْنَعُ بِهِ؟ قَالَ: **«أَتُحِبُّونَ أَنَّهُ لَكُمْ؟»** قَالُوا: وَاللَّهِ لَوْ كَانَ حَيًّا كَانَ عَيْبًا فِيهِ؛ لِأَنَّهُ أَسَكُّ، فَكَيْفَ وَهُوَ مَيِّتٌ؟ فَقَالَ: **«فَوَاللَّهِ لَلدُّنْيَا أَهْوَنُ عَلَى اللَّهِ مِنْ هَذَا عَلَيْكُمْ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Jabir ibn 'Abdullah (رضي الله عنهما).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Prophet ﷺ passed through the marketplace with people surrounding him. He passed by a dead, defective earless kid (young goat). He took hold of its ear and asked: "Which of you would love to buy this for a single Dirham?" They said: "We would not want it for anything; what would we do with it?" He asked: "Would you like it if it were given to you for free?" They said: "By Allah, even if it were alive it would be defective because of its deformed ears, so how about now when it is dead carcass?!" The Prophet ﷺ said: "By Allah, the world is more insignificant in the sight of Allah than this carcass is to you!"
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 2957 (Kitab az-Zuhd war-Raqa'iq, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Sensational physical object lesson demonstrating the utter worthlessness of worldly delusion compared to eternal spiritual realities.

### Hadith #0757
* **Topic / Chapter:** The World is the Prison of the Believer and Paradise of the Disbeliever / الدُّنْيَا سِجْنُ الْمُؤْمِنِ وَجَنَّةُ الْكَافِرِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«الدُّنْيَا سِجْنُ الْمُؤْمِنِ، وَجَنَّةُ الْكَافِرِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "The worldly life is the prison of the believer and the paradise of the disbeliever."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 2956 (Kitab az-Zuhd war-Raqa'iq, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** The believer is restrained by moral boundaries and longs for the boundless felicity of Paradise, while the disbeliever enjoys their only paradise before eternal ruin.

### Hadith #0758
* **Topic / Chapter:** Renouncing Worldly Craving Brings the Love of Allah and People / ازْهَدْ فِي الدُّنْيَا يُحِبَّكَ اللَّهُ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ سَهْلِ بْنِ سَعْدٍ السَّاعِدِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: أَتَى النَّبِيَّ ﷺ رَجُلٌ فَقَالَ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، دُلَّنِي عَلَى عَمَلٍ إِذَا أَنَا عَمِلْتُهُ أَحَبَّنِيَ اللَّهُ وَأَحَبَّنِيَ النَّاسُ، فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«ازْهَدْ فِي الدُّنْيَا يُحِبَّكَ اللَّهُ، وَازْهَدْ فِيمَا فِي أَيْدِي النَّاسِ يُحِبَّكَ النَّاسُ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Sahl ibn Sa'd as-Sa'idi (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > A man came to the Prophet ﷺ and said: "O Messenger of Allah, direct me to an action which, if I perform it, Allah will love me and people will love me." The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Renounce attachment to the world (practise Zuhd), and Allah will love you; and renounce craving what is in the hands of people, and people will love you."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sunan Ibn Majah 4102 (Sahih according to an-Nawawi and al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Detachment from worldly obsession earns divine love, while respecting people's possessions and avoiding greed secures communal respect and affection.

### Hadith #0759
* **Topic / Chapter:** Parable of the Rider Resting Under the Shade of a Tree / مَالِي وَلِلدُّنْيَا، مَا أَنَا وَالدُّنْيَا إِلَّا كَرَاكِبٍ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ مَسْعُودٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: نَامَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ عَلَى حَصِيرٍ، فَقَامَ وَقَدْ أَثَّرَ فِي جَنْبِهِ، فَقُلْنَا: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، لَوِ اتَّخَذْنَا لَكَ وِطَاءً! فَقَالَ: **«مَا لِي وَلِلدُّنْيَا؟ مَا أَنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا إِلَّا كَرَاكِبٍ اسْتَظَلَّ تَحْتَ شَجَرَةٍ، ثُمَّ رَاحَ وَتَرَكَهَا»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ slept on a rough reed mat, and he got up with its marks imprinted on his side. We said: "O Messenger of Allah, if only you allowed us to prepare a soft mattress for you!" He said: "What do I have to do with this world? I am in this world like nothing but a rider who sought shade under a tree for a brief hour, and then departed and left it behind."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2377, Sunan Ibn Majah 4109 (Sahih according to at-Tirmidhi and al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** The Prophet's absolute asceticism and contentment: viewing earthly life as a momentary pause under the shade of a tree during a long journey.

### Hadith #0760
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Insatiable Greed of the Son of Adam for Wealth / لَوْ كَانَ لِابْنِ آدَمَ وَادِيَانِ مِنْ مَالٍ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ: أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: **«لَوْ أَنَّ لِابْنِ آدَمَ وَادِيًا مِنْ ذَهَبٍ أَحَبَّ أَنْ يَكُونَ لَهُ وَادِيَانِ، وَلَنْ يَمْلَأَ عَيْنَهُ إِلَّا التُّرَابُ، وَيَتُوبُ اللَّهُ عَلَى مَنْ تَابَ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Anas ibn Malik (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "If the son of Adam had a valley full of gold, he would desire to have two valleys; and nothing will ever fill his mouth/eyes except the dust (of the grave). And Allah accepts the repentance of the one who repents."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6439, Sahih Muslim 1048 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Material accumulation is an unquenchable thirst that only concludes with death, unless disciplined by spiritual contentment and repentance.

### Hadith #0761
* **Topic / Chapter:** Two Hungry Wolves Among Sheep / مَا ذِئْبَانِ جَائِعَانِ أُرْسِلَا فِي غَنَمٍ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ كَعْبِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ الْأَنْصَارِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«مَا ذِئْبَانِ جَائِعَانِ أُرْسِلَا فِي غَنَمٍ بِأَفْسَدَ لَهَا مِنْ حِرْصِ الْمَرْءِ عَلَى الْمَالِ وَالشَّرَفِ لِدِينِهِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Ka'b ibn Malik (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Two hungry wolves let loose among a flock of sheep do not cause more destructive corruption to them than a person's craving for wealth and social prestige causes to his religion."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2376, Sunan ad-Darimi 2730 (Sahih according to at-Tirmidhi and al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Obsessive ambition for wealth and status ravages religious integrity faster than savage wolves slaughter defenseless sheep.

### Hadith #0762
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Real Fear: Competition for Worldly Abundance / التَّحْذِيرُ مِنَ التَّنَافُسِ فِي الدُّنْيَا
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ عَمْرِو بْنِ عَوْفٍ الْأَنْصَارِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ: أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: **«فَوَاللَّهِ لَا الْفَقْرَ أَخْشَى عَلَيْكُمْ، وَلَكِنْ أَخْشَى عَلَيْكُمْ أَنْ تُبْسَطَ عَلَيْكُمُ الدُّنْيَا كَمَا بُسِطَتْ عَلَى مَنْ كَانَ قَبْلَكُمْ، فَتَنَافَسُوهَا كَمَا تَنَافَسُوهَا، وَتُهْلِكَكُمْ كَمَا أَهْلَكَتْهُمْ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'Amr ibn 'Awf al-Ansari (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "By Allah, it is not poverty that I fear for you; rather, what I fear for you is that worldly wealth will be opened wide for you as it was opened for those before you, and you will compete with one another for it as they competed for it, and it will destroy you as it destroyed them."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 3158, Sahih Muslim 2961 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Unchecked consumerism and materialistic rivalry dissolve faith, sever brotherhood, and bring moral ruin upon prosperous civilizations.

### Hadith #0763
* **Topic / Chapter:** Look at Those Below You in Worldly Wealth / انْظُرُوا إِلَى مَنْ هُوَ أَسْفَلَ مِنْكُمْ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«انْظُرُوا إِلَى مَنْ هُوَ أَسْفَلَ مِنْكُمْ، وَلَا تَنْظُرُوا إِلَى مَنْ هُوَ فَوْقَكُمْ؛ فَهُوَ أَجْدَرُ أَنْ لَا تَزْدَرُوا نِعْمَةَ اللَّهِ عَلَيْكُمْ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Look at those who are beneath you (in material wealth and status), and do not look at those who are above you; for that is more conducive to not belittling the favors of Allah upon you."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6490, Sahih Muslim 2963 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** The psychological master-cure for ingratitude: looking at the less fortunate inspires perpetual thankfulness for divine blessings.

### Hadith #0764
* **Topic / Chapter:** Possessing the Essentials of Daily Life is Equal to Owning the Entire World / مَنْ أَصْبَحَ آمِنًا فِي سِرْبِهِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ سَلَمَةَ بْنِ عُبَيْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ مِحْصَنٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«مَنْ أَصْبَحَ مِنْكُمْ آمِنًا فِي سِرْبِهِ، مُعَافًى فِي جَسَدِهِ، عِنْدَهُ قُوتُ يَوْمِهِ، فَكَأَنَّمَا حِيزَتْ لَهُ الدُّنْيَا بِحَذَافِيرِهَا»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'Ubaydullah ibn Mihsan (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Whoever among you wakes up in the morning secure in his dwelling, healthy in his body, possessing his daily sustenance—it is as though the entire world with all its treasures has been gathered for him!"
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2346, Sunan Ibn Majah 4141 (Hasan according to at-Tirmidhi and al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Hasan (حسن).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Safety, physical health, and simple daily bread constitute the true essence of earthly wealth.

### Hadith #0765
* **Topic / Chapter:** Moderation in Eating: One-Third Food, Drink, and Air / حَسْبُ ابْنِ آدَمَ لُقَيْمَاتٌ يُقِمْنَ صُلْبَهُ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**

### Hadith #0778

It will be asked: 'O son of Adam, did you ever experience any misery? Did you ever suffer any hardship?' He will say: 'No, by Allah, my Lord! ### Hadith #0780

### Hadith #0797
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Fear of Allah in Solitude / الْخَوْفُ مِنَ اللَّهِ فِي الْخَلَوَاتِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ ثَوْبَانَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ أَنَّهُ قَالَ: **«لَأَعْلَمَنَّ أَقْوَامًا مِنْ أُمَّتِي يَأْتُونَ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ بِحَسَنَاتٍ أَمْثَالِ جِبَالِ تِهَامَةَ بِيضًا، فَيَجْعَلُهَا اللَّهُ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ هَبَاءً مَنْثُورًا»**، قَالَ ثَوْبَانُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، صِفْهُمْ لَنَا، جَلِّهِمْ لَنَا أَنْ لَا نَكُونَ مِنْهُمْ وَنَحْنُ لَا نَعْلَمُ، قَالَ: **«أَمَا إِنَّهُمْ إِخْوَانُكُمْ، وَمِنْ جِلْدَتِكُمْ، وَيَأْخُذُونَ مِنَ اللَّيْلِ كَمَا تَأْخُذُونَ، وَلَكِنَّهُمْ أَقْوَامٌ إِذَا خَلَوْا بِمَحَارِمِ اللَّهِ انْتَهَكُوهَا»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Thawban (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**

Thawban pleaded: "O Messenger of Allah, describe them to us so that we may not be among them unknowingly!" He replied: "They are your brothers, from your own race, praying at night as you pray; but when they are in private solitude with the prohibitions of Allah, they violate them."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sunan Ibn Majah 4245 (Sahih according to al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** True piety is tested when one is completely alone in secret; violating divine limits in private dissolves outward public deeds.

### Hadith #0798
