# 13. كِتَابُ الْبِرِّ وَالصِّلَةِ
# Chapter 13: The Book of Piety, Goodness & Kinship

### Hadith #0602
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Mother Deserves the Highest Companionship / مَنْ أَحَقُّ النَّاسِ بِحُسْنِ الصَّحَابَةِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: جَاءَ رَجُلٌ إِلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﷺ فَقَالَ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، مَنْ أَحَقُّ النَّاسِ بِحُسْنِ صَحَابَتِي؟ قَالَ: **«أُمُّكَ»**، قَالَ: ثُمَّ مَنْ؟ قَالَ: **«ثُمَّ أُمُّكَ»**، قَالَ: ثُمَّ مَنْ؟ قَالَ: **«ثُمَّ أُمُّكَ»**، قَالَ: ثُمَّ مَنْ؟ قَالَ: **«ثُمَّ أَبُوكَ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > A man came to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and asked: "O Messenger of Allah, who among mankind is most deserving of my good companionship and devotion?" The Prophet ﷺ replied: "Your mother." The man asked: "Then who?" He replied: "Then your mother." The man asked: "Then who?" He replied: "Then your mother." The man asked: "Then who?" He replied: "Then your father."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 5971, Sahih Muslim 2548 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** The mother is accorded threefold priority in kindness, honor, and service due to the unparalleled hardships of pregnancy, labor, and nursing.

### Hadith #0603
* **Topic / Chapter:** Devotion to Parents Takes Precedence Over Voluntary Jihad / جِهَادُ بِرِّ الْوَالِدَيْنِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَمْرٍو رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: جَاءَ رَجُلٌ إِلَى النَّبِيِّ ﷺ يَسْتَأْذِنُهُ فِي الْجِهَادِ، فَقَالَ: **«أَحَيٌّ وَالِدَاكَ؟»** قَالَ: نَعَمْ، قَالَ: **«فَفِيهِمَا فَجَاهِدْ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn 'Amr (رضي الله عنهما).
* **English Translation:**
  > A man came to the Prophet ﷺ asking permission to participate in Jihad. The Prophet ﷺ asked him: "Are your parents alive?" He replied: "Yes." The Prophet ﷺ said: "Then upon serving them let your striving (Jihad) be!"
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 3004, Sahih Muslim 2549 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Caring for elderly parents and comforting their hearts is an exalted, primary form of Jihad that takes priority over voluntary military campaigns.

### Hadith #0604
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Lord's Pleasure Lies in the Pleasure of the Parents / رِضَا الرَّبِّ فِي رِضَا الْوَالِدِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَمْرٍو رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ: **«رِضَا الرَّبِّ فِي رِضَا الْوَالِدِ، وَسَخَطُ الرَّبِّ فِي سَخَطِ الْوَالِدِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn 'Amr (رضي الله عنهما).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Prophet ﷺ said: "The pleasure of the Lord is in the pleasure of the parent, and the wrath of the Lord is in the wrath of the parent."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Jami' at-Tirmidhi 1899, Sahih Ibn Hibban 429 (Sahih according to at-Tirmidhi and al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Attaining divine favor is fundamentally tied to treating parents with honor, obedience, and gentleness.

### Hadith #0605
* **Topic / Chapter:** Humiliation for the One Who Neglects Aging Parents / رَغِمَ أَنْفُ مَنْ أَدْرَكَ وَالِدَيْهِ عِنْدَ الْكِبَرِ فَلَمْ يَدْخُلِ الْجَنَّةَ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ: **«رَغِمَ أَنْفُ، ثُمَّ رَغِمَ أَنْفُ، ثُمَّ رَغِمَ أَنْفُ»** قِيلَ: مَنْ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ؟ قَالَ: **«مَنْ أَدْرَكَ أَبَوَيْهِ عِنْدَ الْكِبَرِ، أَحَدَهُمَا أَوْ كِلَيْهِمَا، فَلَمْ يَدْخُلِ الْجَنَّةَ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Prophet ﷺ said: "May he be disgraced, then disgraced, and disgraced!" It was asked: "Who, O Messenger of Allah?" He replied: "The one whose parents—one or both of them—reach old age during his lifetime, and yet he fails to enter Paradise (through serving them)."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 2551 (Kitab al-Birr was-Silah wal-Adab, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Caring for elderly parents is an effortless, golden highway leading directly to Paradise; neglecting them is an unforgivable spiritual failure.

### Hadith #0606
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Father is the Central Gateway to Paradise / الْوَالِدُ أَوْسَطُ أَبْوَابِ الْجَنَّةِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي الدَّرْدَاءِ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ يَقُولُ: **«الْوَالِدُ أَوْسَطُ أَبْوَابِ الْجَنَّةِ؛ فَإِنْ شِئْتَ فَأَضِعْ ذَلِكَ الْبَابَ، أَوِْ احْفَظْهُ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu al-Darda' (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > Abu al-Darda' reported: I heard the Messenger of Allah ﷺ say: "The father is the middle gate of Paradise; so if you wish, lose that gate or preserve it."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Jami' at-Tirmidhi 1900, Sunan Ibn Majah 3663 (Sahih according to at-Tirmidhi and al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Honoring, obeying, and respecting one's father secures the best and most spacious portal into the eternal gardens of Paradise.

### Hadith #0607
* **Topic / Chapter:** Filial Piety Ranks Right After Prayer on Time / بِرُّ الْوَالِدَيْنِ بَعْدَ الصَّلَاةِ عَلَى وَقْتِهَا
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ مَسْعُودٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: سَأَلْتُ النَّبِيَّ ﷺ: أَيُّ الْعَمَلِ أَحَبُّ إِلَى اللَّهِ؟ قَالَ: **«الصَّلَاةُ عَلَى وَقْتِهَا»**، قَالَ: ثُمَّ أَيٌّ؟ قَالَ: **«ثُمَّ بِرُّ الْوَالِدَيْنِ»**، قَالَ: ثُمَّ أَيٌّ؟ قَالَ: **«الْجِهَادُ فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > 'Abdullah ibn Mas'ud asked the Prophet ﷺ: "Which deed is most beloved to Allah?" He replied: "Prayer performed at its appointed time." I asked: "Then which?" He said: "Then filial piety towards parents." I asked: "Then which?" He said: "Then Jihad in the path of Allah."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 527, Sahih Muslim 85 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Filial devotion to mother and father stands immediately after the primary pillar of prayer in divine preference.

### Hadith #0608
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Maternal Aunt Holds the Station of the Mother / الْخَالَةُ بِمَنْزِلَةِ الْأُمِّ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنِ الْبَرَاءِ بْنِ عَازِبٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ: **«الْخَالَةُ بِمَنْزِلَةِ الْأُمِّ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Al-Bara' ibn 'Azib (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Prophet ﷺ said: "The maternal aunt is in the position and station of the mother (in kindness, affection, and custody)."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 2699, Jami' at-Tirmidhi 1904 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Honoring, visiting, and serving one's maternal aunt mirrors the religious obligation and spiritual rewards of filial devotion to the mother.

### Hadith #0609
* **Topic / Chapter:** Maintaining Ties with the Friends of One's Father / صِلَةُ أَهْلِ وُدِّ الْأَبِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُمَرَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا: أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: **«إِنَّ أَبَرَّ الْبِرِّ صِلَةُ الْوَلَدِ أَهْلَ وُدِّ أَبِيهِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn 'Umar (رضي الله عنهما).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Indeed, the highest form of filial piety is for a child to maintain ties with the friends and loved ones of his father after he is gone."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 2552 (Kitab al-Birr was-Silah wal-Adab, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Honoring the close companions and friends of deceased parents is an ongoing manifestation of posthumous filial devotion.

### Hadith #0610
* **Topic / Chapter:** Maintaining Kinship Expands Provision and Extends Lifespan / صِلَةُ الرَّحِمِ مَبْسَطَةٌ لِلرِّزْقِ مَنْسَأَةٌ فِي الْأَثَرِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: **«مَنْ أَحَبَّ أَنْ يُبْسَطَ لَهُ فِي رِزْقِهِ، وَيُنْسَأَ لَهُ فِي أَثَرِهِ، فَلْيَصِلْ رَحِمَهُ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Anas ibn Malik (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Whoever loves to have his provision expanded and his lifespan extended (in barakah and legacy), let him uphold the ties of kinship."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 5986, Sahih Muslim 2557 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Maintaining warm family ties brings tangible divine barakah into financial earnings and bestows a long, fruitful life and enduring legacy.

### Hadith #0611
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Sacred Covenant of the Womb (Ar-Rahim) with the Throne / الرَّحِمُ مُعَلَّقَةٌ بِالْعَرْشِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهَا قَالَتْ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«الرَّحِمُ مُعَلَّقَةٌ بِالْعَرْشِ تَقُولُ: مَنْ وَصَلَنِي وَصَلَهُ اللَّهُ، وَمَنْ قَطَعَنِي قَطَعَهُ اللَّهُ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'A'ishah (رضي الله عنها).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "The womb (kinship) is suspended from the Throne of Allah, proclaiming: 'Whoever maintains me, Allah will maintain him; and whoever severs me, Allah will sever him!'"
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 5989, Sahih Muslim 2555 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Upholding ties with relatives guarantees divine connection and mercy, while severing them invites spiritual abandonment from Allah.

### Hadith #0612
* **Topic / Chapter:** The True Upholder of Kinship Reconnects When Severed / لَيْسَ الْوَاصِلُ بِالْمُكَافِئِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَمْرٍو رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ: **«لَيْسَ الْوَاصِلُ بِالْمُكَافِئِ، وَلَكِنِ الْوَاصِلُ الَّذِي إِذَا قُطِعَتْ رَحِمُهُ وَصَلَهَا»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn 'Amr (رضي الله عنهما).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Prophet ﷺ said: "The true upholder of kinship is not the one who merely reciprocates (visits only when visited); rather, the true upholder of kinship is the one who, when his family ties are severed, reconnects them."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 5991 (Kitab al-Adab, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Genuine spiritual nobility is demonstrated by taking the initiative to forgive, visit, and embrace estranged relatives.

### Hadith #0613
* **Topic / Chapter:** Maintaining Ties with Mistreating Relatives / صِلَةُ الرَّحِمِ الْمُسِيئَةِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ: أَنَّ رَجُلًا قَالَ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، إِنَّ لِي قَرَابَةً أَصِلُهُمْ وَيَقْطَعُونِي، وَأُحْسِنُ إِلَيْهِمْ وَيُسِيئُونَ إِلَيَّ، وَأَحْلُمُ عَنْهُمْ وَيَجْهَلُونَ عَلَيَّ، فَقَالَ: **«لَئِنْ كُنْتَ كَمَا قُلْتَ، فَكَأَنَّمَا تُسِفُّهُمُ الْمَلَّ، وَلَا يَزَالُ مَعَكَ مِنَ اللَّهِ ظَهِيرٌ عَلَيْهِمْ مَا دُمْتَ عَلَى ذَلِكَ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > A man said: "O Messenger of Allah, I have relatives with whom I try to maintain ties while they sever them; I do good to them while they mistreat me; and I am patient with them while they act harshly against me." The Prophet ﷺ said: "If you are as you say, it is as if you are feeding them hot ashes (while they bear the sin), and you will not cease to have an appointed supporter from Allah against them as long as you remain on that course."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 2558 (Kitab al-Birr was-Silah wal-Adab, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Responding to family hostility with persistent benevolence grants divine protection and places the moral high ground firmly with the peacemaker.

### Hadith #0614
* **Topic / Chapter:** Charity to Relatives Yields a Double Reward / الصَّدَقَةُ عَلَى الْقَرِيبِ صَدَقَةٌ وَصِلَةٌ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ سَلْمَانَ بْنِ عَامِرٍ الضَّبِّيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ: **«الصَّدَقَةُ عَلَى الْمِسْكِينِ صَدَقَةٌ، وَهِيَ عَلَى ذِي الرَّحِمِ ثِنْتَانِ: صَدَقَةٌ وَصِلَةٌ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Salman ibn 'Amir ad-Dabbi (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Prophet ﷺ said: "Charity given to a poor person is counted as charity, while charity given to a relative is counted as two things: charity and maintaining the ties of kinship."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Jami' at-Tirmidhi 658, Sunan an-Nasa'i 2582 (Sahih according to at-Tirmidhi and al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Supporting impoverished family members earns double credit by fulfilling economic relief and cementing sacred kinship bonds.

### Hadith #0615
* **Topic / Chapter:** Continuous Exhortation Regarding the Neighbor / مَا زَالَ جِبْرِيلُ يُوصِينِي بِالْجَارِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ وَعَائِشَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمْ قَالَا: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«مَا زَالَ جِبْرِيلُ يُوصِينِي بِالْجَارِ، حَتَّى ظَنَنْتُ أَنَّهُ سَيُوَرِّثُهُ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn 'Umar and 'A'ishah (رضي الله عنهم).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Jibril kept advising me so emphatically concerning the rights of the neighbor that I thought he would soon declare him an heir."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6014, Sahih Muslim 2624 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Kindness, patience, and honoring the rights of neighbors is a fundamental pillar of Islamic social ethics emphasized by archangel Jibril.

### Hadith #0616
* **Topic / Chapter:** Negating True Faith from the One Whose Neighbor is Unsafe / لَا يُؤْمِنُ مَنْ لَا يَأْمَنُ جَارُهُ بَوَائِقَهُ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: **«وَاللَّهِ لَا يُؤْمِنُ، وَاللَّهِ لَا يُؤْمِنُ، وَاللَّهِ لَا يُؤْمِنُ»**، قِيلَ: وَمَنْ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ؟ قَالَ: **«الَّذِي لَا يَأْمَنُ جَارُهُ بَوَائِقَهُ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ swore: "By Allah, he does not truly believe! By Allah, he does not truly believe! By Allah, he does not truly believe!" It was asked: "Who, O Messenger of Allah?" He replied: "The one whose neighbor is not safe from his harm and malice."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6016, Sahih Muslim 46 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Harming neighbors through noise, property encroachment, or bad character fundamentally undermines the authenticity of one's faith.

### Hadith #0617
* **Topic / Chapter:** Eating One's Fill While the Neighbor Goes Hungry / لَيْسَ الْمُؤْمِنُ الَّذِي يَشْبَعُ وَجَارُهُ جَائِعٌ

* **Topic / Chapter:** Intercession for Goodness and Mediation of Disputes / الشَّفَاعَةُ فِي الْخَيْرِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي مُوسَى الْأَشْعَرِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: كَانَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ إِذَا جَاءَهُ السَّائِلُ أَوْ طُلِبَتْ إِلَيْهِ حَاجَةٌ قَالَ: **«اشْفَعُوا تُؤْجَرُوا، وَيَقْضِي اللَّهُ عَلَى لِسَانِ نَبِيِّهِ ﷺ مَا شَاءَ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Musa al-Ash'ari (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > When a petitioner or someone with a need came to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, he would turn to his companions and say: "Intercede (for him) and you will be rewarded, and Allah will decree through the tongue of His Prophet what He wills."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 1432, Sahih Muslim 2627 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Using one's influence and status to advocate for the needy and facilitate good causes is an independently rewarded virtue.

### Hadith #0646
* **Topic / Chapter:** Concealing the Faults of a Muslim in This World / مَنْ سَتَرَ مُسْلِمًا سَتَرَهُ اللَّهُ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ: **«لَا يَسْتُرُ عَبْدٌ عَبْدًا فِي الدُّنْيَا، إِلَّا سَتَرَهُ اللَّهُ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Prophet ﷺ said: "No servant conceals the private faults of another servant in this world, except that Allah will conceal his faults on the Day of Resurrection."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 2590 (Kitab al-Birr was-Silah wal-Adab, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Shielding others from public scandal guarantees divine concealment of one's own secret shortcomings on Judgment Day.

### Hadith #0647
* **Topic / Chapter:** Relieving Worldly Distresses of Believers / مَنْ نَفَّسَ عَنْ مُؤْمِنٍ كُرْبَةً
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«مَنْ نَفَّسَ عَنْ مُؤْمِنٍ كُرْبَةً مِنْ كُرَبِ الدُّنْيَا، نَفَّسَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ كُرْبَةً مِنْ كُرَبِ يَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ، وَمَنْ يَسَّرَ عَلَى مُعْسِرٍ، يَسَّرَ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ، وَمَنْ سَتَرَ مُسْلِمًا، سَتَرَهُ اللَّهُ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ، وَاللَّهُ فِي عَوْنِ الْعَبْدِ مَا كَانَ الْعَبْدُ فِي عَوْنِ أَخِيهِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Whoever relieves a believer of one of the distresses of this world, Allah will relieve him of one of the distresses of the Day of Resurrection. Whoever eases the hardship of a debtor in difficulty, Allah will ease his hardship in this world and the Hereafter. Whoever conceals the faults of a Muslim, Allah will conceal his faults in this world and the Hereafter. And Allah remains in the aid of His servant as long as the servant remains in the aid of his brother."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 2699 (Kitab adh-Dhikr wad-Du'a' wat-Tawbah, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Immediate divine assistance, worldly relief, and eternal salvation are directly tied to helping vulnerable fellow human beings.

### Hadith #0648
* **Topic / Chapter:** Hospitality to the Guest / مَنْ كَانَ يُؤْمِنُ بِاللَّهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الْآخِرِ فَلْيُكْرِمْ ضَيْفَهُ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي شُرَيْحٍ الْخُزَاعِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«مَنْ كَانَ يُؤْمِنُ بِاللَّهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الْآخِرِ فَلْيُكْرِمْ ضَيْفَهُ جَائِزَتَهُ»**، قَالُوا: وَمَا جَائِزَتُهُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ؟ قَالَ: **«يَوْمُهُ وَلَيْلَتُهُ، وَالضِّيَافَةُ ثَلَاثَةُ أَيَّامٍ، فَمَا كَانَ وَرَاءَ ذَلِكَ فَهُوَ صَدَقَةٌ عَلَيْهِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Shurayh al-Khuza'i (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day, let him honor his guest with his special hospitality (Ja'izatah)." They asked: "What is his special hospitality, O Messenger of Allah?" He replied: "His first day and night; and general hospitality is for three days. Whatever is beyond that is an act of charity upon him."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6135, Sahih Muslim 48 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Generous hospitality to travelers and visitors is a binding hallmark of faith, with special lavishness for 24 hours and basic care for three days.

### Hadith #0649
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Superiority of Kindness to Non-Muslim Parents / صِلَةُ الْوَالِدَيْنِ الْمُشْرِكَيْنِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَسْمَاءَ بِنْتِ أَبِي بَكْرٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَتْ: قَدِمَتْ عَلَيَّ أُمِّي وَهِيَ مُشْرِكَةٌ فِي عَهْدِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﷺ، فَاسْتَفْتَيْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قُلْتُ: إِنَّ أُمِّي قَدِمَتْ وَهِيَ رَاغِبَةٌ، أَفَأَصِلُ أُمِّي؟ قَالَ: **«نَعَمْ، صِلِي أُمَّكِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Asma' bint Abi Bakr (رضي الله عنهما).
* **English Translation:**
  > Asma' bint Abi Bakr reported: My mother came to visit me while she was still a polytheist during the treaty of the Prophet ﷺ. I asked the Messenger of Allah ﷺ for guidance: "My mother has arrived desiring my affection/help; shall I maintain ties with my mother?" He replied: "Yes, maintain ties of kindness with your mother."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 2620, Sahih Muslim 1003 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Differences in religious faith never abolish the sacred moral duty of maintaining compassionate filial kindness to parents.

### Hadith #0650
* **Topic / Chapter:** Relieving the Debtor by Granting Respite or Debt Forgiveness / إِنْظَارُ الْمُعْسِرِ أَوْ وَضْعُ الدَّيْنِ عَنْهُ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ حُذَيْفَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«أُتِيَ اللَّهُ بِعَبْدٍ مِنْ عِبَادِهِ آتَاهُ اللَّهُ مَالًا، فَقَالَ لَهُ: مَاذَا عَمِلْتَ فِي الدُّنْيَا؟ قَالَ: يَا رَبِّ، آتَيْتَنِي مَالَكَ، فَكُنْتُ أُبَايِعُ النَّاسَ، وَكَانَ مِنْ خُلُقِي الْجَوَازُ، فَكُنْتُ أَتَيَسَّرُ عَلَى الْمُوسِرِ، وَأُنْظِرُ الْمُعْسِرَ، فَقَالَ اللَّهُ: أَنَا أَحَقُّ بِذَلِكَ مِنْكَ؛ تَجَاوَزُوا عَنْ عَبْدِي»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Hudhayfah ibn al-Yaman (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "A man from among Allah's servants was brought before Allah, to whom Allah had given wealth. Allah asked him: 'What did you do in the world?' He said: 'O Lord, You gave me Your wealth, and I used to trade with people. It was my habit to be lenient: I was easy on the well-off and gave extra time to the insolvent debtor.' Allah said: 'I have more right to leniency than you; overlook and forgive the sins of My servant!'"
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 2077, Sahih Muslim 1560 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Treating financially burdened borrowers with patience and forgiveness secures absolute divine leniency and pardon on Judgment Day.

### Hadith #0651
* **Topic / Chapter:** Sincere Mutual Advice (An-Nasihah) is the Essence of Religion / الدِّينُ النَّصِيحَةُ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ تَمِيمٍ الدَّارِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ ﷺ قَالَ: **«الدِّينُ النَّصِيحَةُ»**، قُلْنَا: لِمَنْ؟ قَالَ: **«لِلَّهِ، وَلِكِتَابِهِ، وَلِرَسُولِهِ، وَلِأَئِمَّةِ الْمُسْلِمِينَ وَعَامَّتِهِمْ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Tamim ad-Dari (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Prophet ﷺ said: "The Religion is sincere goodwill and advice (An-Nasihah)." We asked: "To whom?" He replied: "To Allah, to His Book, to His Messenger, and to the leaders of the Muslims and their common folk."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 55 (Kitab al-Iman, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Genuine faith expresses itself through sincere devotion to Allah, honoring His revelation, loving His Prophet, and actively working for the well-being of the entire community.
