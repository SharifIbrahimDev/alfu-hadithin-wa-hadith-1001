# Chapter 18: Book of Transactions, Halal Earning & Financial Integrity
# كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ

### Hadith #0852
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: The Honest and Trustworthy Merchant / التَّاجِرُ الصَّدُوقُ الأَمِينُ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ أَبِي سَعِيدٍ الْخُدْرِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: «التَّاجِرُ الصَّدُوقُ الأَمِينُ مَعَ النَّبِيِّينَ وَالصِّدِّيقِينَ وَالشُّهَدَاءِ».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Abu Sa'id al-Khudri (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** The Prophet ﷺ said: "The truthful and trustworthy merchant will be associated with the Prophets, the upright (Siddiqin), and the martyrs on the Day of Judgment."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sunan at-Tirmidhi (1209), Sunan ad-Darimi (2539) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih li-Ghayrihi (Al-Albani).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Engaging in trade with scrupulous honesty, truthfulness, and transparency elevates commercial enterprise into high worship, ranking the ethical merchant alongside the spiritual elite of humanity.

### Hadith #0853
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: The Best and Purest Forms of Earning / أَطْيَبُ الْكَسْبِ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ رَافِعِ بْنِ خَدِيجٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قِيلَ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، أَيُّ الْكَسْبِ أَطْيَبُ؟ قَالَ: «عَمَلُ الرَّجُلِ بِيَدِهِ، وَكُلُّ بَيْعٍ مَبْرُورٍ».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Rafi' ibn Khadij (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** It was asked: "O Messenger of Allah, which earning is the best and purest?" He ﷺ replied: "A man's work with his own hands, and every honest, accepted business transaction (free of fraud, deceit, and interest)."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Musnad Ahmad (17265), Al-Mustadrak 'ala al-Sahihayn by al-Hakim (2160) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (Al-Albani, Sahih at-Targhib 1691).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Islam honors productive physical labor, trade craftsmanship, and ethical commerce that avoids deception and unlawful exploitation.

### Hadith #0854
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Divine Mercy for Leniency in Buying, Selling, and Demanding Rights / السَّمَاحَةُ فِي الْبَيْعِ وَالشِّرَاءِ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ جَابِرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: «رَحِمَ اللَّهُ رَجُلًا سَمْحًا إِذَا بَاعَ، وَإِذَا اشْتَرَى، وَإِذَا اقْتَضَى».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Jabir ibn 'Abdullah (may Allah be pleased with them both)  
**[English Translation]:** The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "May Allah show mercy to a person who is lenient and easygoing when he sells, when he buys, and when he demands back his rights (or debts)."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih al-Bukhari (2076) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (صحيح).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Generosity, kindness, and flexibility in financial transactions invite special divine mercy and ease in this life and the Hereafter.

### Hadith #0855
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: The Right of Option (Khiyar al-Majlis) and Blessing in Truthfulness / خِيَارُ الْمَجْلِسِ وَالْبَرَكَةُ فِي الصِّدْقِ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ حَكِيمِ بْنِ حِزَامٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: «الْبَيِّعَانِ بِالْخِيَارِ مَا لَمْ يَتَفَرَّقَا - أَوْ قَالَ: حَتَّى يَتَفَرَّقَا - فَإِنْ صَدَقَا وَبَيَّنَا بُورِكَ لَهُمَا فِي بَيْعِهِمَا، وَإِنْ كَتَمَا وَكَذَبَا مُحِقَتْ بَرَكَةُ بَيْعِهِمَا».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Hakim ibn Hizam (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** The Prophet ﷺ said: "The buyer and the seller have the option to cancel or confirm the deal as long as they have not parted. If both speak the truth and disclose all defects, their transaction will be blessed; but if they conceal defects and tell lies, the blessing of their transaction will be wiped out."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih al-Bukhari (2079), Sahih Muslim (1532) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Barakah in commercial wealth is tied directly to radical transparency and truthfulness; concealing product flaws drains the divine blessing out of any monetary profit.

### Hadith #0856
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: The Clear Boundaries of Halal, Haram, and Doubtful Matters / الْحَلَالُ بَيِّنٌ وَالْحَرَامُ بَيِّنٌ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنِ النُّعْمَانِ بْنِ بَشِيرٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ: «إِنَّ الْحَلَالَ بَيِّنٌ، وَإِنَّ الْحَرَامَ بَيِّنٌ، وَبَيْنَهُمَا مُشْتَبِهَاتٌ لَا يَعْلَمُهُنَّ كَثِيرٌ مِنَ النَّاسِ، فَمَنِ اتَّقَى الشُّبُهَاتِ اسْتَبْرَأَ لِدِينِهِ وَعِرْضِهِ، وَمَنْ وَقَعَ فِي الشُّبُهَاتِ وَقَعَ فِي الْحَرَامِ، كَالرَّاعِي يَرْعَى حَوْلَ الْحِمَى يُوشِكُ أَنْ يَرْتَعَ فِيهِ...».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by An-Nu'man ibn Bashir (may Allah be pleased with them both)  
**[English Translation]:** The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "The lawful is clear and the unlawful is clear, and between them are doubtful matters about which many people do not know. Thus, whoever avoids doubtful matters clears himself in regard to his religion and his honor, but whoever falls into doubtful matters falls into the unlawful, like a shepherd who grazes around a private sanctuary, bordering on grazing within it..."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih al-Bukhari (52), Sahih Muslim (1599) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** A Muslim avoids questionable contracts, ambiguous financial schemes, and borderline earnings to preserve spiritual purity and reputation.

### Hadith #0857
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Leaving Doubt for Certainty in Financial Conduct / تَرْكُ الشُّبُهَاتِ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنِ الْحَسَنِ بْنِ عَلِيٍّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: حَفِظْتُ مِنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «دَعْ مَا يَرِيبُكَ إِلَى مَا لَا يَرِيبُكَ، فَإِنَّ الصِّدْقَ طُمَأْنِينَةٌ، وَإِنَّ الْكَذِبَ رِيبَةٌ».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Al-Hasan ibn 'Ali (may Allah be pleased with them both)  
**[English Translation]:** I memorized from the Messenger of Allah ﷺ: "Leave that which makes you doubt for that which does not cause you doubt; for truthfulness brings inner tranquility, and falsehood brings doubt and unease."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sunan at-Tirmidhi (2518), Sunan an-Nasa'i (5711) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (Al-Albani).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Conscientiousness (Wara') requires relinquishing suspicious transactions or gray-area contracts in favor of unambiguous, unblemished halal income.

### Hadith #0858
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Absolute Prohibition of Fraud and Deception / التَّحْذِيرُ مِنَ الْغِشِّ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم مَرَّ عَلَى صُبْرَةِ طَعَامٍ، فَأَدْخَلَ يَدَهُ فِيهَا فَنَالَتْ أَصَابِعُهُ بَلَلًا، فَقَالَ: «مَا هَذَا يَا صَاحِبَ الطَّعَامِ؟» قَالَ: أَصَابَتْهُ السَّمَاءُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ. قَالَ: «أَفَلَا جَعَلْتَهُ فَوْقَ الطَّعَامِ كَيْ يَرَاهُ النَّاسُ؟ مَنْ غَشَّ فَلَيْسَ مِنِّي».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** The Messenger of Allah ﷺ passed by a pile of grain and inserted his hand into it, and his fingers felt moisture. He said: "What is this, O owner of the food?" The man said: "Rain fell upon it, O Messenger of Allah." He ﷺ said: "Why did you not put it on top of the food so that people could see it? Whoever deceives (cheats) is not of me (does not follow my way)."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih Muslim (102) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (صحيح).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Concealing damaged goods, manipulating packaging, or misleading consumers violates Islamic ethics and removes one from the Prophetic standard of conduct.

### Hadith #0859
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Indifference Toward the Source of Wealth as an End-Times Corrupter / اسْتِحْلَالُ الْمَالِ الْحَرَامِ فِي آخِرِ الزَّمَانِ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: «يَأْتِي عَلَى النَّاسِ زَمَانٌ، لَا يُبَالِي الْمَرْءُ مَا أَخَذَ مِنْهُ، أَمِنَ الْحَلَالِ أَمْ مِنَ الْحَرَامِ».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** The Prophet ﷺ said: "A time will surely come upon the people when a person will not care how he acquires his wealth, whether from halal (lawful) or haram (unlawful) sources."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih al-Bukhari (2059) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (صحيح).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** A warning against material greed overcoming moral boundaries; a true believer constantly audits their revenue streams regardless of prevailing societal corruption.

### Hadith #0860
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Strict Curse upon All Parties to Usury (Riba) / لَعْنُ آكِلِ الرِّبَا وَمُؤْكِلِهِ وَكَاتِبِهِ وَشَاهِدَيْهِ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ جَابِرٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: «لَعَنَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم آكِلَ الرِّبَا، وَمُؤْكِلَهُ، وَكَاتِبَهُ، وَشَاهِدَيْهِ»، وَقَالَ: «هُمْ سَوَاءٌ».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Jabir (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** The Messenger of Allah ﷺ cursed the one who consumes usury (interest), the one who pays it, the one who records it, and the two witnesses to it, and he said: "They are all equal in sin."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih Muslim (1598) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (صحيح).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Riba is among the most destructive sins; culpability extends beyond the primary beneficiary to facilitators, underwriters, and contract scribes.

### Hadith #0861
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: The Core Rules of Exchange in Ribawi Commodities / الْأَصْنَافُ الرِّبَوِيَّةُ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ عُبَادَةَ بْنِ الصَّامِتِ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «الذَّهَبُ بِالذَّهَبِ، وَالْفِضَّةُ بِالْفِضَّةِ، وَالْبُرُّ بِالْبُرِّ، وَالشَّعِيرُ بِالشَّعِيرِ، وَالتَّمْرُ بِالتَّمْرِ، وَالْمِلْحُ بِالْمِلْحِ، مِثْلًا بِمِثْلٍ، سَوَاءً بِسَوَاءٍ، يَدًا بِيَدٍ، فَإِذَا اخْتَلَفَتْ هَذِهِ الْأَصْنَافُ فَبِيعُوا كَيْفَ شِئْتُمْ إِذَا كَانَ يَدًا بِيَدٍ».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by 'Ubadah ibn as-Samit (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Gold for gold, silver for silver, wheat for wheat, barley for barley, dates for dates, and salt for salt—like for like, equal for equal, hand to hand (immediate exchange). But if these categories differ, sell as you wish, provided it is hand to hand."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih Muslim (1587) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (صحيح).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Establishing the foundational economic jurisprudence against interest by requiring exact equivalence and prompt spot settlement in primary commodities and currencies.

### Hadith #0862
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Prohibition of Ambiguous, Speculative, and Deceptive Sales (Gharar) / النَّهْيُ عَنْ بَيْعِ الْغَرَرِ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: «نَهَى رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَنْ بَيْعِ الْحَصَاةِ، وَعَنْ بَيْعِ الْغَرَرِ».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** The Messenger of Allah ﷺ prohibited transactions determined by throwing stones (a gamble-based ancient trade method) and transactions involving excessive uncertainty/ambiguity (Gharar).  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih Muslim (1513) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (صحيح).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Contracts must have transparent specifications of price, quantity, quality, and deliverability, completely void of speculation or deceptive gambles.

### Hadith #0863
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Prohibition of Artificial Bidding to Inflate Prices (Najsh) / النَّهْيُ عَنِ النَّجْشِ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: «نَهَى النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَنِ النَّجْشِ».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn 'Umar (may Allah be pleased with them both)  
**[English Translation]:** The Prophet ﷺ forbade *Najsh* (bidding on a commodity without the intention to buy, merely to artificially inflate its price for others).  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih al-Bukhari (2142), Sahih Muslim (1516) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Shilling, fake online reviews, deceptive price gouging, and collusive bidding are strictly unlawful in Islamic commercial jurisprudence.

### Hadith #0864
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Prohibition of Intercepting Goods Before Reaching Open Markets / النَّهْيُ عَنْ تَلَقِّي الرُّكْبَانِ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: «لَا تَلَقَّوُا الرُّكْبَانَ لِلْبَيْعِ، وَلَا يَبِعْ بَعْضُكُمْ عَلَى بَيْعِ بَعْضٍ، وَلَا تَنَاجَشُوا، وَلَا يَبِعْ حَاضِرٌ لِبَادٍ...».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Do not intercept trade caravans (outside the city) to buy their goods before they know market prices, do not undersell or undercut one another's deals, do not practice artificial bidding (Najsh), and a city resident must not act as a broker to hoard for a desert dweller..."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih al-Bukhari (2150), Sahih Muslim (1515) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Protecting market participants from information asymmetry ensures fair price discovery and prevents middlemen from exploiting unaware producers.

### Hadith #0865
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Respecting Ongoing Negotiations and Transactions / النَّهْيُ عَنِ السَّوْمِ عَلَى سَوْمِ الْأَخِ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُمَرَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: «لَا يَبِعِ الرَّجُلُ عَلَى بَيْعِ أَخِيهِ، وَلَا يَخْطُبْ عَلَى خِطْبَةِ أَخِيهِ، إِلَّا أَنْ يَأْذَنَ لَهُ».

### Hadith #0870

### Hadith #0879
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: The Divine Requirement of Pure Halal Consumption / اللَّهُ طَيِّبٌ لَا يَقْبَلُ إِلَّا طَيِّبًا*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ، إِنَّ اللَّهَ طَيِّبٌ لَا يَقْبَلُ إِلَّا طَيِّبًا، وَإِنَّ اللَّهَ أَمَرَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ بِمَا أَمَرَ بِهِ الْمُرْسَلِينَ... ثُمَّ ذَكَرَ الرَّجُلَ يُطِيلُ السَّفَرَ أَشْعَثَ أَغْبَرَ يَمُدُّ يَدَيْهِ إِلَى السَّمَاءِ: يَا رَبِّ يَا رَبِّ، وَمَطْعَمُهُ حَرَامٌ، وَمَشْرَبُهُ حَرَامٌ، وَمَلْبَسُهُ حَرَامٌ، وَغُذِيَ بِالْحَرَامِ، فَأَنَّى يُسْتَجَابُ لِذَلِكَ!».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "O people! Indeed, Allah is Pure and accepts only that which is pure. And Allah commands the believers with that which He commanded the Messengers... Then he mentioned a man who travels far, disheveled and dusty, raising his hands to the sky supplicating: 'O Lord! O Lord!' while his food is haram, his drink is haram, his clothing is haram, and he is nourished with haram—how then could his prayer be answered?!"  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih Muslim (1015) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (صحيح).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Consuming unlawfully earned income erects an impenetrable barrier against the acceptance of du'a and righteous deeds.

### Hadith #0880
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Procrastination in Repaying Debt by the Solvent is an Injustice (Dhulm) / مَطْلُ الْغَنِيِّ ظُلْمٌ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: «مَطْلُ الْغَنِيِّ ظُلْمٌ، وَإِذَا أُتْبِعَ أَحَدُكُمْ عَلَى مَلِيءٍ فَلْيَتَّبِعْ».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Procrastination in debt repayment by a wealthy (capable) person is an injustice (Dhulm); and if one of you is referred to a solvent person (to collect debt), let him agree to the transfer (Hawalah)."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih al-Bukhari (2287), Sahih Muslim (1564) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Delaying the settlement of debts when one has the financial capacity to pay is a form of oppression against the creditor.

### Hadith #0881
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: The Best of People are Those Who Settle Debts in the Most Generous Manner / خِيَارُكُمْ أَحْسَنُكُمْ قَضَاءً*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، أَنَّ رَجُلًا أَتَى النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَتَقَاضَاهُ بَعِيرًا، فَأَعْطَاهُ سِنًّا فَوْقَ سِنِّهِ، وَقَالَ: «خِيَارُكُمْ أَحْسَنُكُمْ قَضَاءً».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** A man came to the Prophet ﷺ demanding repayment of a camel, so he ﷺ gave him a camel of superior age and quality, saying: "The best among you are those who are best in discharging and repaying their debts."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih al-Bukhari (2305), Sahih Muslim (1601) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Voluntarily repaying a debt with something better or with added courtesy—without prior condition—is a noble Islamic mark of integrity.

### Hadith #0882
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Divine Aid for the Borrower with Sincere Intent to Repay / نِيَّةُ أَدَاءِ الدَّيْنِ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: «مَنْ أَخَذَ أَمْوَالَ النَّاسِ يُرِيدُ أَدَاءَهَا أَدَّى اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، وَمَنْ أَخَذَ يُرِيدُ إِتْلَافَهَا أَتْلَفَهُ اللَّهُ».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever takes people's wealth (as a loan or trust) with the intention to repay it, Allah will facilitate its repayment on his behalf; but whoever takes it intending to destroy/squander it, Allah will destroy him."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih al-Bukhari (2387) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (صحيح).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Sincerity of intention in borrowing invites divine financial assistance, whereas fraudulent default brings ruin in both worlds.

### Hadith #0883
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Massive Reward for Granting Respite to or Relieving a Debtor / إِنْظَارُ الْمُعْسِرِ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ أَبِي قَتَادَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ: «مَنْ سَرَّهُ أَنْ يُنْجِيَهُ اللَّهُ مِنْ كُرَبِ يَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ، فَلْيُنَفِّسْ عَنْ مُعْسِرٍ، أَوْ يَضَعْ عَنْهُ».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Abu Qatadah (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Whoever is pleased that Allah save him from the agonies of the Day of Resurrection, let him give respite to a debtor in financial distress or forgive part or all of his debt."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih Muslim (1563) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (صحيح).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Granting payment extensions or debt waivers to insolvent debtors secures deliverance from the terror and hardship of Judgment Day.

### Hadith #0884
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: The Divine Forgiveness Granted to the Compassionate Lender / التَّجَاوُزُ عَنِ الْمُعْسِرِينَ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ حُذَيْفَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «أَنَّ رَجُلًا مَاتَ فَدَخَلَ الْجَنَّةَ، فَقِيلَ لَهُ: مَا كُنْتَ تَعْمَلُ؟ قَالَ: إِنِّي كُنْتُ أُبَايِعُ النَّاسَ، فَكُنْتُ أُنْظِرُ الْمُعْسِرَ، وَأَتَجَاوَزُ فِي السِّكَّةِ - أَوِ النَّقْدِ - فَغَفَرَ اللَّهُ لَهُ».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Hudhayfah (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** The Prophet ﷺ said: "A man died and entered Paradise. It was said to him: 'What good deed did you do?' He replied: 'I used to engage in business with people and I would grant respite to the debtor in hardship and overlook minor deficiencies in payment.' So Allah forgave him."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih al-Bukhari (2077), Sahih Muslim (1560) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Compassion and flexibility towards struggling customers and debtors earn the ultimate reward of divine forgiveness.

### Hadith #0885
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Daily Charity for Every Day of Respite Granted to a Debtor / فَضْلُ إِنْظَارِ الْمُعْسِرِ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ سُلَيْمَانَ بْنِ بُرَيْدَةَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ: «مَنْ أَنْظَرَ مُعْسِرًا فَلَهُ بِكُلِّ يَوْمٍ مِثْلِهِ صَدَقَةٌ»، ثُمَّ سَمِعْتُهُ يَقُولُ: «مَنْ أَنْظَرَ مُعْسِرًا فَلَهُ بِكُلِّ يَوْمٍ مِثْلَيْهِ صَدَقَةٌ»، قُلْتُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، سَمِعْتُكَ تَقُولُ كَذَا ثُمَّ كَذَا! قَالَ: «لَهُ بِكُلِّ يَوْمٍ مِثْلِهِ صَدَقَةٌ قَبْلَ أَنْ يَحِلَّ الدَّيْنُ، فَإِذَا حَلَّ الدَّيْنُ فَأَنْظَرَهُ فَلَهُ بِكُلِّ يَوْمٍ مِثْلَيْهِ صَدَقَةٌ».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Buraydah ibn al-Hasib (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Whoever gives respite to an insolvent debtor will receive the reward of giving charity equal to the amount of the debt for every day before it is due; and once the debt becomes due and he gives him further respite, he will receive the reward of giving double that amount in charity for every day."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Musnad Ahmad (23046), Sunan Ibn Majah (2418) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (Al-Albani, Sahih al-Jami' 6187).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Showing patience to struggling borrowers generates compounding spiritual rewards equivalent to massive daily charitable donations.

### Hadith #0886
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn 'Amr ibn al-'As (may Allah be pleased with them both)

**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih Muslim (1886) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (صحيح).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Financial liabilities owed to fellow humans are not automatically expunged even by the ultimate sacrifice of martyrdom; restitution of people's rights is mandatory.

### Hadith #0887
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**

**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Heirs and executors must prioritize discharging the deceased's debts before distributing inheritances, freeing their soul for divine bliss.

### Hadith #0888

### Hadith #0889
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**

**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih al-Bukhari (2237), Sahih Muslim (1561) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Revenue generated from immoral services, superstitious deception, or impure commodities is intrinsically filth (Khabith) and strictly unlawful.

### Hadith #0890
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**

**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih al-Bukhari (2236), Sahih Muslim (1581) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Any substance or object that is fundamentally prohibited for use or consumption is also strictly forbidden to commercialize, sell, or trade.

### Hadith #0891
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: When Allah Forbids Something, He Forbids Its Monetary Value / تَحْرِيمُ ثَمَنِ الْمُحَرَّمَاتِ*

عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «إِنَّ اللَّهَ إِذَا حَرَّمَ عَلَى قَوْمٍ أَكْلَ شَيْءٍ حَرَّمَ عَلَيْهِمْ ثَمَنَهُ».

**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** A foundational legal maxim in Islamic finance: it is impermissible to profit financially from what the Lawgiver has declared intrinsically harmful and prohibited.

### Hadith #0892
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Permissibility of Collateral / Pledging in Debt Transactions (Ar-Rahn) / مَشْرُوعِيَّةُ الرَّهْنِ*

عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهَا: «أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم اشْتَرَى طَعَامًا مِنْ يَهُودِيٍّ إِلَى أَجَلٍ، وَرَهَنَهُ دِرْعًا مِنْ حَدِيدٍ».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by 'A'ishah (may Allah be pleased with her)  
**[English Translation]:** The Prophet ﷺ purchased food supplies from a Jewish merchant on deferred payment, and he pledged his iron coat of armor with him as collateral (Rahn).  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih al-Bukhari (2068), Sahih Muslim (1603) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Securing loans with physical collateral is permissible in Islamic transactions, as is dealing fairly in business with non-Muslims.

### Hadith #0893
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Communal Shared Resources Must Not Be Monopolized / النَّاسُ شُرَكَاءُ فِي ثَلَاثٍ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «الْمُسْلِمُونَ شُرَكَاءُ فِي ثَلَاثٍ: فِي الْمَاءِ، وَالْكَلَإِ، وَالنَّارِ، وَثَمَنُهُ حَرَامٌ».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn 'Abbas (may Allah be pleased with them both)  
**[English Translation]:** The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Muslims are equal partners in three essential public goods: water, pasture, and fire (energy/fuel); and trading or monopolizing their common source is unlawful."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sunan Abi Dawud (3477), Sunan Ibn Majah (2472) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (Al-Albani, Sahih al-Jami' 6701).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Basic life essentials and natural common resources belong communally to society and must never be private-monopolized to deprive the vulnerable.

### Hadith #0894
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Warning Against Hidden Usurious Buyback Schemes (Al-'Inah) / التَّحْذِيرُ مِنْ بَيْعِ الْعِينَةِ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ: «إِذَا تَبَايَعْتُمْ بِالْعِينَةِ، وَأَخَذْتُمْ أَذْنَابَ الْبَقَرِ، وَرَضِيتُمْ بِالزَّرْعِ، وَتَرَكْتُمُ الْجِهَادَ، سَلَّطَ اللَّهُ عَلَيْكُمْ ذُلًّا لَا يَنْزِعُهُ حَتَّى تَرْجِعُوا إِلَى دِينِكُمْ».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn 'Umar (may Allah be pleased with them both)  
**[English Translation]:** The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "When you engage in 'Inah sales (disguised usury buyback schemes), pursue the tails of cattle, become content with agriculture alone, and abandon striving in the cause of Allah, Allah will inflict upon you a humiliation that He will not remove until you return to your religion."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sunan Abi Dawud (3462), Musnad Ahmad (5562) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (Al-Albani).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Resorting to legal subterfuges (Hiyal) to legitimize interest contracts and prioritizing material comfort over religious defense invites communal weakness and disgrace.

### Hadith #0895
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Prohibition of Selling Stolen Goods / شِرَاءُ الْمَالِ الْمَغْصُوبِ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: «مَنِ اشْتَرَى سَرِقَةً وَهُوَ يَعْلَمُ أَنَّهَا سَرِقَةٌ، فَقَدِ اشْتَرَكَ فِي عَارِهَا وَإِثْمِهَا».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever purchases stolen property knowing that it is stolen has shared in its shame and its sin."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Al-Mustadrak 'ala al-Sahihayn by al-Hakim (2253), Sunan al-Bayhaqi (10834) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (Al-Albani, Sahih al-Jami' 6185).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Knowingly buying stolen, illicit, or unlawfully confiscated goods makes the buyer an active accessory to theft and corruption.

### Hadith #0896
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Prohibition of Fraudulent Claims and Bribery (Rishwah) / لَعْنُ الرَّاشِي وَالْمُرْتَشِي*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَمْرٍو رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: «لَعَنَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم الرَّاشِيَ وَالْمُرْتَشِيَ».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn 'Amr (may Allah be pleased with them both)  
**[English Translation]:** The Messenger of Allah ﷺ cursed the one who offers a bribe and the one who accepts it.  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sunan Abi Dawud (3580), Sunan at-Tirmidhi (1337), Sunan Ibn Majah (2313) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (Al-Albani).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Bribery corrupts judicial and administrative systems, robs deserving citizens of their rights, and incurs direct expulsion from divine mercy.

### Hadith #0897
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: The Right of Pre-emption (Shuf'ah) to Prevent Harm Between Partners / ثُبُوتُ حَقِّ الشُّفْعَةِ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ جَابِرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: «قَضَى رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِالشُّفْعَةِ فِي كُلِّ مَا لَمْ يُقْسَمْ، فَإِذَا وَقَعَتِ الْحُدُودُ وَصُرِّفَتِ الطُّرُقُ فَلَا شُفْعَةَ».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Jabir ibn 'Abdullah (may Allah be pleased with them both)  
**[English Translation]:** The Messenger of Allah ﷺ decreed the right of pre-emption (*Shuf'ah*) in all joint undivided property; but once boundaries are demarcated and roads are paved, pre-emption no longer applies.  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih al-Bukhari (2213), Sahih Muslim (1608) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Pre-emption safeguards existing co-owners from suffering harm or undesirable partners in joint real estate.

### Hadith #0898
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Returning Lost or Misplaced Items (Luqatah) with Full Announcement / حُكْمُ اللُّقَطَةِ وَأَمَانَتُهَا*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ خَالِدٍ الْجُهَنِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: سُئِلَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَنْ لُقَطَةِ الذَّهَبِ أَوِ الْوَرِقِ، فَقَالَ: «اعْرِفْ وِكَاءَهَا وَعِفَاصَهَا، ثُمَّ عَرِّفْهَا سَنَةً، فَإِنْ لَمْ تُعْرَفْ فَاسْتَنْفِقْهَا، وَلْتَكُنْ وَدِيعَةً عِنْدَكَ، فَإِنْ جَاءَ طَالِبُهَا يَوْمًا مِنَ الدَّهْرِ فَأَدِّهَا إِلَيْهِ».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Zayd ibn Khalid al-Juhani (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** The Messenger of Allah ﷺ was asked about finding lost gold or silver. He said: "Recognize its strap and its container, then announce it publicly for one full year. If its owner is not identified, you may spend it as a trust; if the seeker arrives any day in the future, pay it back to him."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih al-Bukhari (2426), Sahih Muslim (1722) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Preserving lost property with meticulous care and public announcement demonstrates the sanctity of private property in Islam.

### Hadith #0899
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Prohibition of Taking Official Gifts as Concealed Bribery (Ghulul) / هَدَايَا الْعُمَّالِ غُلُولٌ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ أَبِي حُمَيْدٍ السَّاعِدِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم اسْتَعْمَلَ ابْنَ اللُّتْبِيَّةِ عَلَى الصَّدَقَةِ، فَلَمَّا قَدِمَ قَالَ: هَذَا لَكُمْ وَهَذَا أُهْدِيَ لِي. فَقَامَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَحَمِدَ اللَّهَ وَأَثْنَى عَلَيْهِ ثُمَّ قَالَ: «مَا بَالُ عَامِلٍ أَبْعَثُهُ فَيَقُولُ: هَذَا لَكُمْ وَهَذَا أُهْدِيَ لِي! أَفَلَا قَعَدَ فِي بَيْتِ أَبِيهِ أَوْ فِي بَيْتِ أُمِّهِ حَتَّى يَنْظُرَ أَيُهْدَى إِلَيْهِ أَمْ لَا؟!».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Abu Humayd as-Sa'idi (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** The Prophet ﷺ appointed a man named Ibn al-Lutbiyyah to collect zakah. When he returned, he said: "This is for you (the treasury), and this was gifted to me personally." The Prophet ﷺ stood up, praised Allah, and said: "What is wrong with an official whom I send and he says: 'This is for you and this is gifted to me!' Why did he not sit in the house of his father or mother to see if gifts would be presented to him or not?!"  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sahih al-Bukhari (7174), Sahih Muslim (1832) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Public servants, corporate procurement agents, and auditors are forbidden from accepting gifts associated with their official position, as it constitutes corrupt breach of trust (Ghulul).

### Hadith #0900
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: Fulfilling All Contractual Agreements and Covenants / الْمُسْلِمُونَ عِنْدَ شُرُوطِهِمْ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ عَمْرِو بْنِ عَوْفٍ الْمُزَنِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: «الصُّلْحُ جَائِزٌ بَيْنَ الْمُسْلِمِينَ، إِلَّا صُلْحًا حَرَّمَ حَلَالًا، أَوْ أَحَلَّ حَرَامًا، وَالْمُسْلِمُونَ عَلَى شُرُوطِهِمْ، إِلَّا شَرْطًا حَرَّمَ حَلَالًا، أَوْ أَحَلَّ حَرَامًا».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by 'Amr ibn 'Awf al-Muzani (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Reconciliation is permissible between Muslims, except a settlement that makes lawful what is forbidden or forbids what is lawful. And Muslims are strictly bound by their conditions (and covenants), except a condition that makes lawful the forbidden or forbids the lawful."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sunan at-Tirmidhi (1352), Sunan Abi Dawud (3594) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (Al-Albani).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Pacta sunt servanda: Muslims must scrupulously fulfill business terms, service-level agreements, and covenants unless they contradict clear Islamic law.

### Hadith #0901
**[Topic / Chapter: Book of Transactions & Halal Earning / كِتَابُ الْمُعَامَلَاتِ وَالْبُيُوعِ وَالْكَسْبِ الْحَلَالِ]**  
*Sub-topic: The Obligation of Paying Back What is Borrowed / الْعَارِيَّةُ مُؤَدَّاةٌ*

**[Arabic Matn with Tashkeel]:**  
عَنْ أَبِي أُمَامَةَ الْبَاهِلِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ فِي خُطْبَتِهِ عَامَ حَجَّةِ الْوَدَاعِ: «الْعَارِيَّةُ مُؤَدَّاةٌ، وَالْمِنْحَةُ مَرْدُودَةٌ، وَالدَّيْنُ مَقْضِيٌّ، وَالزَّعِيمُ غَارِمٌ».

**[Narrator]:** Narrated by Abu Umamah al-Bahili (may Allah be pleased with him)  
**[English Translation]:** The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said in his Farewell Sermon: "A borrowed item must be returned, a gifted milking animal should be returned, a debt must be settled, and a guarantor (Kafeel) is liable for payment."  
**[Reference & Grading]:** Sunan at-Tirmidhi (1265), Sunan Abi Dawud (3565) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (Al-Albani).  
**[Key Benefit / Takeaway]:** Comprehensive financial accountability: borrowed items must be restored in full, debts repaid, and commercial guarantees honored without dispute.
