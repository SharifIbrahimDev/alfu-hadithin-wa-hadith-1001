### Hadith #0352 | الحديث رقم: 0352

#### 🇸🇦 القسم العربي (Arabic Text & Takhrij)
<div dir="rtl" align="right">

##### **[الباب: خَيْرُكُمْ مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ الْقُرْآنَ وَعَلَّمَهُ]**

> **نَصُّ الْحَدِيثِ بِالتَّشْكِيلِ:**  
> عَنْ عُثْمَانَ بْنِ عَفَّانَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ: **«خَيْرُكُمْ مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ الْقُرْآنَ وَعَلَّمَهُ»**.

* **الرَّاوِي:** عُثْمَانُ بْنُ عَفَّانَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ.
* **التَّخْرِيجُ:** صحيح البخاري (رقم 5027) — المكتبة الشاملة.
* **الْحُكْمُ:** صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)

</div>

#### 🇬🇧 English Translation & Commentary
##### **[Topic: The Best of People is the One Who Learns the Qur'an and Teaches It]**

> **English Translation:**  
> *"The Prophet ﷺ said: "The best among you are those who learn the Qur'an and teach it."*

* **Companion Narrator:** Narrated by 'Uthman ibn 'Affan (رضي الله عنه).
* **Canonical Reference:** صحيح البخاري (رقم 5027) — المكتبة الشاملة.
* **Scholarly Grading:** **صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)**
* **Key Lessons & Takeaways:** The highest nobility and virtue in this Ummah is attained through devotion to learning Allah's Book, mastering its meanings, and transmitting its knowledge to others.

### Hadith #0353 | الحديث رقم: 0353

#### 🇸🇦 القسم العربي (Arabic Text & Takhrij)
<div dir="rtl" align="right">

##### **[الباب: مَثَلُ الْمُؤْمِنِ الَّذِي يَقْرَأُ الْقُرْآنَ]**

> **نَصُّ الْحَدِيثِ بِالتَّشْكِيلِ:**  
> عَنْ أَبِي مُوسَى الْأَشْعَرِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«مَثَلُ الْمُؤْمِنِ الَّذِي يَقْرَأُ الْقُرْآنَ كَمَثَلِ الْأُتْرُجَّةِ؛ رِيحُهَا طَيِّبٌ وَطَعْمُهَا طَيِّبٌ، وَمَثَلُ الْمُؤْمِنِ الَّذِي لَا يَقْرَأُ الْقُرْآنَ كَمَثَلِ التَّمْرَةِ؛ لَا رِيحَ لَهَا وَطَعْمُهَا حُلْوٌ، وَمَثَلُ الْمُنَافِقِ الَّذِي يَقْرَأُ الْقُرْآنَ مَثَلُ الرَّيْحَانَةِ؛ رِيحُهَا طَيِّبٌ وَطَعْمُهَا مُرٌّ، وَمَثَلُ الْمُنَافِقِ الَّذِي لَا يَقْرَأُ الْقُرْآنَ كَمَثَلِ الْحَنْظَلَةِ؛ لَيْسَ لَهَا رِيحٌ وَطَعْمُهَا مُرٌّ»**.

* **الرَّاوِي:** أَبُو مُوسَى الْأَشْعَرِيُّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ.
* **التَّخْرِيجُ:** صحيح البخاري (رقم 5427)، وصحيح مسلم (رقم 797) — متفق عليه.
* **الْحُكْمُ:** صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)

</div>

#### 🇬🇧 English Translation & Commentary
##### **[Topic: Parable of the Believer Who Recites the Qur'an]**

> **English Translation:**  
> *"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "The example of a believer who recites the Qur'an is like that of a citron (utrujjah) whose fragrance is sweet and taste is delicious. The example of a believer who does not recite the Qur'an is like that of a date; it has no fragrance but its taste is sweet. The example of a hypocrite who recites the Qur'an is like that of basil (rayhanah); its fragrance is sweet but its taste is bitter. And the example of a hypocrite who does not recite the Qur'an is like that of a colocynth (hanzalah); it has no fragrance and its taste is bitter."*

* **Companion Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Musa al-Ash'ari (رضي الله عنه).
* **Canonical Reference:** صحيح البخاري (رقم 5427)، وصحيح مسلم (رقم 797) — متفق عليه.
* **Scholarly Grading:** **صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)**
* **Key Lessons & Takeaways:** Recitation of the Qur'an enriches the believer's inner faith and adorns their outer manifestation with blessings and fragrant impact upon others.

### Hadith #0354 | الحديث رقم: 0354

#### 🇸🇦 القسم العربي (Arabic Text & Takhrij)
<div dir="rtl" align="right">

##### **[الباب: الْمَاهِرُ بِالْقُرْآنِ مَعَ السَّفَرَةِ الْكِرَامِ الْبَرَرَةِ]**

> **نَصُّ الْحَدِيثِ بِالتَّشْكِيلِ:**  
> عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهَا قَالَتْ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«الْمَاهِرُ بِالْقُرْآنِ مَعَ السَّفَرَةِ الْكِرَامِ الْبَرَرَةِ، وَالَّذِي يَقْرَأُ الْقُرْآنَ وَيَتَتَعْتَعُ فِيهِ، وَهُوَ عَلَيْهِ شَاقٌّ، لَهُ أَجْرَانِ»**.

* **الرَّاوِي:** أُمُّ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ عَائِشَةُ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهَا.
* **التَّخْرِيجُ:** صحيح مسلم (رقم 798)، وصحيح البخاري (رقم 4937) — متفق عليه.
* **الْحُكْمُ:** صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)

</div>

#### 🇬🇧 English Translation & Commentary
##### **[Topic: The Expert in the Qur'an and the One Who Struggles in Recitation]**

> **English Translation:**  
> *"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "The one who is proficient in the Qur'an will be with the noble, righteous scribes (angels), and the one who recites the Qur'an and stammers in it, finding it difficult, will have a double reward."*

* **Companion Narrator:** Narrated by 'A'ishah (رضي الله عنها).
* **Canonical Reference:** صحيح مسلم (رقم 798)، وصحيح البخاري (رقم 4937) — متفق عليه.
* **Scholarly Grading:** **صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)**
* **Key Lessons & Takeaways:** Stumbling during Qur'anic recitation should never discourage a person; Allah rewards both the recitation itself and the sincere effort expended to recite it correctly.

##### **[الباب: فَضْلُ الزَّهْرَاوَيْنِ: الْبَقَرَةِ وَآلِ عِمْرَانَ]**

* **الرَّاوِي:** أَبُو أُمَامَةَ الْبَاهِلِيُّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ.

* **الْحُكْمُ:** صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)

</div>

#### 🇬🇧 English Translation & Commentary
##### **[Topic: Virtues of Az-Zahrawan (Surat al-Baqarah and Ali 'Imran)]**

> **English Translation:**  
> *"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Recite the two luminous Surahs: Al-Baqarah and Surah Ali 'Imran, for they will come on the Day of Resurrection like two clouds, or two shades, or two flocks of birds spreading their wings, pleading on behalf of their companions. Recite Surah Al-Baqarah, for taking it is a blessing, abandoning it is a cause of grief, and the sorcerers (al-batalah) cannot withstand it."*

* **Companion Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Umamah al-Bahili (رضي الله عنه).
* **Canonical Reference:** صحيح مسلم (رقم 804) — المكتبة الشاملة.
* **Scholarly Grading:** **صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)**
* **Key Lessons & Takeaways:** Surat al-Baqarah and Ali 'Imran provide shade, divine advocacy, and absolute spiritual protection against sorcery and evil forces.

### Hadith #0359 | الحديث رقم: 0359

#### 🇸🇦 القسم العربي (Arabic Text & Takhrij)
<div dir="rtl" align="right">

##### **[الباب: آيَةُ الْكُرْسِيِّ أَعْظَمُ آيَةٍ فِي كِتَابِ اللَّهِ]**

> **نَصُّ الْحَدِيثِ بِالتَّشْكِيلِ:**  
> عَنْ أُبَيِّ بْنِ كَعْبٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«يَا أَبَا الْمُنْذِرِ، أَتَدْرِي أَيُّ آيَةٍ مِنْ كِتَابِ اللَّهِ مَعَكَ أَعْظَمُ؟»** قَالَ: قُلْتُ: اللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُ أَعْلَمُ. قَالَ: **«يَا أَبَا الْمُنْذِرِ أَتَدْرِي أَيُّ آيَةٍ مِنْ كِتَابِ اللَّهِ مَعَكَ أَعْظَمُ؟»** قَالَ: قُلْتُ: ﴿اللَّهُ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ﴾. قَالَ: فَضَرَبَ فِي صَدْرِي، وَقَالَ: **«وَاللَّهِ لِيَهْنِكَ الْعِلْمُ أَبَا الْمُنْذِرِ»**.

* **الرَّاوِي:** أُبَيُّ بْنُ كَعْبٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ.
* **التَّخْرِيجُ:** صحيح مسلم (رقم 810) — المكتبة الشاملة.
* **الْحُكْمُ:** صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)

</div>

#### 🇬🇧 English Translation & Commentary
##### **[Topic: Ayat al-Kursi is the Greatest Verse in the Qur'an]**

> **English Translation:**  
> *"Ubayy ibn Ka'b narrated that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ asked: "O Abu al-Mundhir, do you know which verse from the Book of Allah that you have with you is the greatest?" I replied: "Allah and His Messenger know best." He asked again: "O Abu al-Mundhir, do you know which verse from the Book of Allah that you have with you is the greatest?" I said: "﴿Allāhu lā ilāha illā Huwa al-Ḥayyu al-Qayyūm﴾ (Ayat al-Kursi, 2:255)." Thereupon he struck my chest and said: "By Allah, congratulations on your knowledge, O Abu al-Mundhir!"*

* **Companion Narrator:** Narrated by Ubayy ibn Ka'b (رضي الله عنه).
* **Canonical Reference:** صحيح مسلم (رقم 810) — المكتبة الشاملة.
* **Scholarly Grading:** **صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)**
* **Key Lessons & Takeaways:** Ayat al-Kursi is the greatest verse of the Qur'an because it encompasses the sublime Attributes of Allah's Oneness, absolute Sovereignty, eternal Life, and all-encompassing Sustenance.

* **الرَّاوِي:** أُمُّ سَلَمَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهَا.
* **التَّخْرِيجُ:** سنن أبي داود (رقم 4001)، وجامع الترمذي (رقم 2927) — صحيح.
* **الْحُكْمُ:** صَحِيحٌ (Sahih)

</div>

* **Companion Narrator:** Narrated by Umm Salamah (رضي الله عنها).

##### **[Topic: Satan Weeps When the Reciter Prostrates]**

> **English Translation:**  
> *"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "When the son of Adam recites a verse of prostration and prostrates, Satan withdraws weeping, saying: 'Woe to me! The son of Adam was commanded to prostrate and he prostrated, so Paradise is his; whereas I was commanded to prostrate and I refused, so the Fire is mine."*
