# 08. كِتَابُ فَضَائِلِ الْقُرْآنِ
# Chapter 08: The Book of Virtues of the Qur'an

---

### Hadith #0352
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Best of People is the One Who Learns the Qur'an and Teaches It / خَيْرُكُمْ مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ الْقُرْآنَ وَعَلَّمَهُ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ عُثْمَانَ بْنِ عَفَّانَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ: **«خَيْرُكُمْ مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ الْقُرْآنَ وَعَلَّمَهُ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'Uthman ibn 'Affan (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Prophet ﷺ said: "The best among you are those who learn the Qur'an and teach it."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 5027 (Kitab Fada'il al-Qur'an, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** The highest nobility and virtue in this Ummah is attained through devotion to learning Allah's Book, mastering its meanings, and transmitting its knowledge to others.

### Hadith #0353
* **Topic / Chapter:** Parable of the Believer Who Recites the Qur'an / مَثَلُ الْمُؤْمِنِ الَّذِي يَقْرَأُ الْقُرْآنَ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي مُوسَى الْأَشْعَرِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«مَثَلُ الْمُؤْمِنِ الَّذِي يَقْرَأُ الْقُرْآنَ كَمَثَلِ الْأُتْرُجَّةِ؛ رِيحُهَا طَيِّبٌ وَطَعْمُهَا طَيِّبٌ، وَمَثَلُ الْمُؤْمِنِ الَّذِي لَا يَقْرَأُ الْقُرْآنَ كَمَثَلِ التَّمْرَةِ؛ لَا رِيحَ لَهَا وَطَعْمُهَا حُلْوٌ، وَمَثَلُ الْمُنَافِقِ الَّذِي يَقْرَأُ الْقُرْآنَ مَثَلُ الرَّيْحَانَةِ؛ رِيحُهَا طَيِّبٌ وَطَعْمُهَا مُرٌّ، وَمَثَلُ الْمُنَافِقِ الَّذِي لَا يَقْرَأُ الْقُرْآنَ كَمَثَلِ الْحَنْظَلَةِ؛ لَيْسَ لَهَا رِيحٌ وَطَعْمُهَا مُرٌّ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Musa al-Ash'ari (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "The example of a believer who recites the Qur'an is like that of a citron (utrujjah) whose fragrance is sweet and taste is delicious. The example of a believer who does not recite the Qur'an is like that of a date; it has no fragrance but its taste is sweet. The example of a hypocrite who recites the Qur'an is like that of basil (rayhanah); its fragrance is sweet but its taste is bitter. And the example of a hypocrite who does not recite the Qur'an is like that of a colocynth (hanzalah); it has no fragrance and its taste is bitter."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 5427, Sahih Muslim 797 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Recitation of the Qur'an enriches the believer's inner faith and adorns their outer manifestation with blessings and fragrant impact upon others.

### Hadith #0354
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Expert in the Qur'an and the One Who Struggles in Recitation / الْمَاهِرُ بِالْقُرْآنِ مَعَ السَّفَرَةِ الْكِرَامِ الْبَرَرَةِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهَا قَالَتْ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«الْمَاهِرُ بِالْقُرْآنِ مَعَ السَّفَرَةِ الْكِرَامِ الْبَرَرَةِ، وَالَّذِي يَقْرَأُ الْقُرْآنَ وَيَتَتَعْتَعُ فِيهِ، وَهُوَ عَلَيْهِ شَاقٌّ، لَهُ أَجْرَانِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'A'ishah (رضي الله عنها).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "The one who is proficient in the Qur'an will be with the noble, righteous scribes (angels), and the one who recites the Qur'an and stammers in it, finding it difficult, will have a double reward."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 4937, Sahih Muslim 798 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Stumbling during Qur'anic recitation should never discourage a person; Allah rewards both the recitation itself and the sincere effort expended to recite it correctly.

### Hadith #0355
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Qur'an Interceding on the Day of Resurrection / الْقُرْآنُ يَأْتِي يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ شَفِيعًا لِأَصْحَابِهِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي أُمَامَةَ الْبَاهِلِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ يَقُولُ: **«اقْرَءُوا الْقُرْآنَ؛ فَإِنَّهُ يَأْتِي يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ شَفِيعًا لِأَصْحَابِهِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Umamah al-Bahili (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Recite the Qur'an, for it will come on the Day of Resurrection as an intercessor for its companions."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 804 (Kitab Salat al-Musafirin wa Qasruha, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Regular recitation, contemplation, and implementation of the Qur'an secures its advocacy on the Day of Judgment when humanity is in dire need of intercession.

### Hadith #0356
* **Topic / Chapter:** Virtues of Az-Zahrawan (Surat al-Baqarah and Ali 'Imran) / فَضْلُ الزَّهْرَاوَيْنِ: الْبَقَرَةِ وَآلِ عِمْرَانَ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي أُمَامَةَ الْبَاهِلِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ يَقُولُ: **«اقْرَءُوا الزَّهْرَاوَيْنِ: الْبَقَرَةَ وَسُورَةَ آلِ عِمْرَانَ؛ فَإِنَّهُمَا تَأْتِيَانِ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ كَأَنَّهُمَا غَمَامَتَانِ، أَوْ كَأَنَّهُمَا غَيَايَتَانِ، أَوْ كَأَنَّهُمَا فِرْقَانِ مِنْ طَيْرٍ صَوَافَّ، تُحَاجَّانِ عَنْ أَصْحَابِهِمَا، اقْرَءُوا سُورَةَ الْبَقَرَةِ؛ فَإِنَّ أَخْذَهَا بَرَكَةٌ، وَتَرْكَهَا حَسْرَةٌ، وَلَا تَسْتَطِيعُهَا الْبَطَلَةُ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Umamah al-Bahili (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Recite the two luminous Surahs: Al-Baqarah and Surah Ali 'Imran, for they will come on the Day of Resurrection like two clouds, or two shades, or two flocks of birds spreading their wings, pleading on behalf of their companions. Recite Surah Al-Baqarah, for taking it is a blessing, abandoning it is a cause of grief, and the sorcerers (al-batalah) cannot withstand it."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 804 (Kitab Salat al-Musafirin wa Qasruha, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Surat al-Baqarah and Ali 'Imran provide shade, divine advocacy, and absolute spiritual protection against sorcery and evil forces.

### Hadith #0357
* **Topic / Chapter:** Surat al-Baqarah Expels Satan from the Home / سُورَةُ الْبَقَرَةِ تَطْرُدُ الشَّيْطَانَ مِنَ الْبَيْتِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: **«لَا تَجْعَلُوا بُيُوتَكُمْ مَقَابِرَ، إِنَّ الشَّيْطَانَ يَنْفِرُ مِنَ الْبَيْتِ الَّذِي تُقْرَأُ فِيهِ سُورَةُ الْبَقَرَةِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Do not make your houses into graveyards. Indeed, Satan flees from the house in which Surah Al-Baqarah is recited."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 780 (Kitab Salat al-Musafirin wa Qasruha, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Illuminating homes with Qur'anic recitation—especially Surat al-Baqarah—purifies living spaces and keeps diabolical whispers away.

### Hadith #0358
* **Topic / Chapter:** Virtue of the Last Two Verses of Surat al-Baqarah / فَضْلُ الْآيَتَيْنِ مِنْ آخِرِ سُورَةِ الْبَقَرَةِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي مَسْعُودٍ الْبَدْرِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ النَّبِيُّ ﷺ: **«مَنْ قَرَأَ بِالْآيَتَيْنِ مِنْ آخِرِ سُورَةِ الْبَقَرَةِ فِي لَيْلَةٍ كَفَتَاهُ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Mas'ud al-Badri (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever recites the last two verses of Surah Al-Baqarah at night, they will suffice him."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 5009, Sahih Muslim 808 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** The final two verses of Surat al-Baqarah (2:285-286) suffice the reciter against all harm and fulfill the night's essential spiritual need for protection.

### Hadith #0359
* **Topic / Chapter:** Ayat al-Kursi is the Greatest Verse in the Qur'an / آيَةُ الْكُرْسِيِّ أَعْظَمُ آيَةٍ فِي كِتَابِ اللَّهِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أُبَيِّ بْنِ كَعْبٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«يَا أَبَا الْمُنْذِرِ، أَتَدْرِي أَيُّ آيَةٍ مِنْ كِتَابِ اللَّهِ مَعَكَ أَعْظَمُ؟»** قَالَ: قُلْتُ: اللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُ أَعْلَمُ. قَالَ: **«يَا أَبَا الْمُنْذِرِ أَتَدْرِي أَيُّ آيَةٍ مِنْ كِتَابِ اللَّهِ مَعَكَ أَعْظَمُ؟»** قَالَ: قُلْتُ: ﴿اللَّهُ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ﴾. قَالَ: فَضَرَبَ فِي صَدْرِي، وَقَالَ: **«وَاللَّهِ لِيَهْنِكَ الْعِلْمُ أَبَا الْمُنْذِرِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Ubayy ibn Ka'b (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > Ubayy ibn Ka'b narrated that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ asked: "O Abu al-Mundhir, do you know which verse from the Book of Allah that you have with you is the greatest?" I replied: "Allah and His Messenger know best." He asked again: "O Abu al-Mundhir, do you know which verse from the Book of Allah that you have with you is the greatest?" I said: "﴿Allāhu lā ilāha illā Huwa al-Ḥayyu al-Qayyūm﴾ (Ayat al-Kursi, 2:255)." Thereupon he struck my chest and said: "By Allah, congratulations on your knowledge, O Abu al-Mundhir!"
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 810 (Kitab Salat al-Musafirin wa Qasruha, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Ayat al-Kursi is the greatest verse of the Qur'an because it encompasses the sublime Attributes of Allah's Oneness, absolute Sovereignty, eternal Life, and all-encompassing Sustenance.

### Hadith #0360
* **Topic / Chapter:** Protection from the Dajjal Through Surat al-Kahf / عِصْمَةٌ مِنْ فِتْنَةِ الدَّجَّالِ بِآيَاتِ سُورَةِ الْكَهْفِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي الدَّرْدَاءِ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ ﷺ قَالَ: **«مَنْ حَفِظَ عَشْرَ آيَاتٍ مِنْ أَوَّلِ سُورَةِ الْكَهْفِ عُصِمَ مِنَ الدَّجَّالِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu al-Darda' (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever memorizes ten verses from the beginning of Surah Al-Kahf will be protected from the trial of the Dajjal (Antichrist)."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 809 (Kitab Salat al-Musafirin wa Qasruha, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Memorizing and reflecting on the opening verses of Surat al-Kahf grants firm spiritual insight and steadfastness against the greatest deception in human history.

### Hadith #0361
* **Topic / Chapter:** Light from Friday to Friday Through Surat al-Kahf / نُورٌ بَيْنَ الْجُمُعَتَيْنِ بِقِرَاءَةِ سُورَةِ الْكَهْفِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي سَعِيدٍ الْخُدْرِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ ﷺ قَالَ: **«مَنْ قَرَأَ سُورَةَ الْكَهْفِ فِي يَوْمِ الْجُمُعَةِ أَضَاءَ لَهُ مِنَ النُّورِ مَا بَيْنَ الْجُمُعَتَيْنِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Sa'id al-Khudri (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever recites Surah Al-Kahf on Friday, light will shine for him between the two Fridays."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** As-Sunan al-Kubra by an-Nasa'i 10790, Al-Mustadrak by al-Hakim 2/368 (Sahih according to al-Albani in Sahih al-Jami' 6470, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Reciting Surat al-Kahf every Friday illuminates the believer's path with divine guidance, discernment, and peace for the entire following week.

### Hadith #0362
* **Topic / Chapter:** Surat al-Ikhlas Equals One-Third of the Qur'an / سُورَةُ الْإِخْلَاصِ تَعْدِلُ ثُلُثَ الْقُرْآنِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي سَعِيدٍ الْخُدْرِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ: أَنَّ رَجُلًا سَمِعَ رَجُلًا يَقْرَأُ: ﴿قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ﴾ يُرَدِّدُهَا، فَلَمَّا أَصْبَحَ جَاءَ إِلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﷺ فَذَكَرَ ذَلِكَ لَهُ، وَكَأَنَّ الرَّجُلَ يَتَقَالُّهَا، فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بِيَدِهِ إِنَّهَا لَتَعْدِلُ ثُلُثَ الْقُرْآنِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Sa'id al-Khudri (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > A man heard another man reciting: ﴿Say: He is Allah, the One﴾ (Surah Al-Ikhlas) repeating it over and over. In the morning, he came to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and mentioned that to him, seemingly thinking it was too little. The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "By the One in Whose Hand is my soul, it is truly equivalent to one-third of the Qur'an!"
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 5013 (Kitab Fada'il al-Qur'an, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Surat al-Ikhlas is equivalent to a third of the Qur'an because the Qur'an addresses Creed (Tawhid), rulings (Ahkam), and narratives (Qasas), and this Surah is purely dedicated to pure Tawhid.

### Hadith #0363
* **Topic / Chapter:** Love for Surat al-Ikhlas Enters Paradise / حُبُّ سُورَةِ الْإِخْلَاصِ يُوجِبُ الْجَنَّةَ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، أَنَّ رَجُلًا مِنَ الْأَنْصَارِ كَانَ يَؤُمُّهُمْ فِي مَسْجِدِ قُبَاءٍ، وَكَانَ كُلَّمَا افْتَتَحَ سُورَةً يَقْرَأُ بِهَا لَهُمْ فِي الصَّلَاةِ قَرَأَ: ﴿قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ﴾، حَتَّى يَفْرُغَ مِنْهَا، ثُمَّ يَقْرَأُ سُورَةً أُخْرَى مَعَهَا... فَقَالَ لَهُ النَّبِيُّ ﷺ: **«يَا فُلَانُ، مَا يَمْنَعُكَ أَنْ تَفْعَلَ مَا يَأْمُرُكَ بِهِ أَصْحَابُكَ؟ وَمَا يَحْمِلُكَ عَلَى لُزُومِ هَذِهِ السُّورَةِ فِي كُلِّ رَكْعَةٍ؟»** فَقَالَ: إِنِّي أُحِبُّهَا، فَقَالَ: **«حُبُّكَ إِيَّاهَا أَدْخَلَكَ الْجَنَّةَ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Anas ibn Malik (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > Anas ibn Malik reported that an Ansari man used to lead prayers at the Quba Mosque. In every Rak'ah, he would first recite Surah Al-Ikhlas and then recite another Surah with it... The Prophet ﷺ asked him: "What prevents you from doing what your companions request, and what makes you adhere to this Surah in every unit of prayer?" He replied: "I truly love it." The Prophet ﷺ said: "Your love for it will admit you into Paradise."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 774 (Kitab al-Adhan, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Loving Allah's Attributes and deriving joy from contemplating His absolute Oneness is a direct path to Paradise.

### Hadith #0364
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Superiority of the Two Surahs of Protection (Al-Mu'awwidhatayn) / فَضْلُ الْمُعَوِّذَتَيْنِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**

### Hadith #0368

### Hadith #0369
* **Topic / Chapter:** Praiseworthy Envy (Ghibtah) for the Bearer of the Qur'an / لَا حَسَدَ إِلَّا فِي اثْنَتَيْنِ: رَجُلٌ آتَاهُ اللَّهُ الْقُرْآنَ

### Hadith #0401
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Qur'an is the Firm Rope of Allah / الْقُرْآنُ حَبْلُ اللَّهِ الْمَتِينُ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ أَرْقَمَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَامَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ يَوْمًا فِينَا خَطِيبًا بِمَاءٍ يُدْعَى خُمًّا... فَحَمِدَ اللَّهَ وَأَثْنَى عَلَيْهِ، وَوَعَظَ وَذَكَّرَ، ثُمَّ قَالَ: **«أَمَّا بَعْدُ، أَلَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ، فَإِنَّمَا أَنَا بَشَرٌ يُوشِكُ أَنْ يَأْتِيَ رَسُولُ رَبِّي فَأُجِيبَ، وَأَنَا تَارِكٌ فِيكُمْ ثَقَلَيْنِ: أَوَّلُهُمَا كِتَابُ اللَّهِ، فِيهِ الْهُدَى وَالنُّورُ، فَخُذُوا بِكِتَابِ اللَّهِ وَاسْتَمْسِكُوا بِهِ»**، فَحَثَّ عَلَى كِتَابِ اللَّهِ وَرَغَّبَ فِيهِ، ثُمَّ قَالَ: **«وَأَهْلُ بَيْتِي؛ أُذَكِّرُكُمُ اللَّهَ فِي أَهْلِ بَيْتِي»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Zayd ibn Arqam (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > Zayd ibn Arqam (رضي الله عنه) reported: One day the Messenger of Allah ﷺ stood up to deliver a sermon at a spring of water called Khumm... He praised and glorified Allah, exhorted and reminded us, and then said: "Now to proceed: Behold, O people! I am only a human being, and it is near that the messenger of my Lord (the Angel of Death) comes and I answer the call. I am leaving among you two weighty matters: the first is the Book of Allah, in which there is guidance and light; so hold fast to the Book of Allah and adhere strictly to it." He encouraged adherence to the Book of Allah and inspired love for it, and then said: "And my household; I remind you of Allah concerning my household."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 2408 (Kitab Fada'il as-Sahabah, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** The Book of Allah is the infallible, everlasting source of guidance and light; holding firmly to it ensures eternal salvation and protection against misguidance.
