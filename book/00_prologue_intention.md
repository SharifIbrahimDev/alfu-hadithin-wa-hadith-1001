# أَلْفُ حَدِيثٍ وَحَدِيثٍ — 1001 Authentic Hadith
## مُقَدِّمَةٌ فِي الْإِخْلَاصِ وَإِحْضَارِ النِّيَّةِ
### Prologue: Sincerity & Rectification of Intention

---

### **الحديث رقم: 0001**
### **Hadith #0001**

#### **[الباب: إنما الأعمال بالنيات]**
#### **[Chapter: Actions are judged only by intentions]**

---

#### **نَصُّ الْحَدِيثِ بِالتَّشْكِيلِ (Arabic Text with Full Diacritics):**

> **عَنْ أَمِيرِ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ أَبِي حَفْصٍ عُمَرَ بْنِ الْخَطَّابِ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ تَعَالَى عَنْهُ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ يَقُولُ:**
> 
> **«إِنَّمَا الأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ، وَإِنَّمَا لِكُلِّ امْرِئٍ مَا نَوَى، فَمَنْ كَانَتْ هِجْرَتُهُ إِلَى اللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِ فَهِجْرَتُهُ إِلَى اللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِ، وَمَنْ كَانَتْ هِجْرَتُهُ لِدُنْيَا يُصِيبُهَا أَوِ امْرَأَةٍ يَنْكِحُهَا فَهِجْرَتُهُ إِلَى مَا هَاجَرَ إِلَيْهِ».**

---

#### **الرَّاوِي (The Narrator):**
**Narrated by the Commander of the Faithful, Abu Hafs 'Umar ibn al-Khattab (may Allah be pleased with him).**

---

#### **التَّرْجَمَةُ الإِنْجِلِيزِيَّةُ (English Translation):**
> "I heard the Messenger of Allah ﷺ say: **'Actions are judged only by intentions, and every person will have only that which he intended. So whoever emigrated for the sake of Allah and His Messenger, his emigration is for Allah and His Messenger; and whoever emigrated for worldly benefit to attain or a woman to marry, his emigration is for that for which he emigrated.'**"

---

#### **التَّخْرِيجُ وَالْحُكْمُ (Reference & Authentic Grading):**
* **Source:** *Sahih al-Bukhari* (Hadith 1, 54, 2529, 3898, 5070, 6689, 6953); *Sahih Muslim* (Hadith 1907).
* **Scholarly Grading:** **مُتَّفَقٌ عَلَيْهِ (Muttafaqun 'Alayh — Agreed Upon by al-Bukhari & Muslim)**.
* **Significance:** Imam ash-Shafi'i and Imam Ahmad said: *"This hadith constitutes one-third of all Islamic knowledge, and enters into seventy chapters of Fiqh."*

---

#### **الْفَوَائِدُ وَالْعِبَرُ (Key Takeaways & Reflections):**
1. **Purity of Intention (*Ikhlas*):** No deed—whether prayer, charity, knowledge, or daily work—is accepted by Allah unless it is done purely and sincerely for His sake.
2. **Transformation of Daily Habits:** Ordinary daily habits (eating, sleeping, working, exercising) become rewarded acts of worship when accompanied by a righteous intention.
3. **The Standard Opener of Knowledge:** Classical scholars traditionally opened their major works with this hadith to remind both the author and the reader to purify their intentions for Allah alone.
