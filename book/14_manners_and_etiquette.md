### Hadith #0652
* **Topic / Chapter:** Spreading the Greeting of Peace to Foster Mutual Love / إِفْشَاءُ السَّلَامِ يُوجِبُ الْمَحَبَّةَ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«لَا تَدْخُلُونَ الْجَنَّةَ حَتَّى تُؤْمِنُوا، وَلَا تُؤْمِنُوا حَتَّى تَحَابُّوا، أَوَلَا أَدُلُّكُمْ عَلَى شَيْءٍ إِذَا فَعَلْتُمُوهُ تَحَابَبْتُمْ؟ أَفْشُوا السَّلَامَ بَيْنَكُمْ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "You will not enter Paradise until you believe, and you will not believe until you love one another. Shall I not guide you to something which, if you do it, you will love one another? Spread the greeting of peace (As-Salam) among yourselves."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 54 (Kitab al-Iman, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Exchanging the Islamic greeting of Salam dismantles social barriers, dispels malice, and builds the mutual love essential for perfected faith.

### Hadith #0653
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Order and Precedence in Giving Salam / مَنْ يُسَلِّمُ عَلَى مَنْ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«يُسَلِّمُ الرَّاكِبُ عَلَى الْمَاشِي، وَالْمَاشِي عَلَى الْقَاعِدِ، وَالْقَلِيلُ عَلَى الْكَثِيرِ، وَالصَّغِيرُ عَلَى الْكَبِيرِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "The rider should greet the walking person, the walking person should greet the seated person, the smaller group should greet the larger group, and the younger should greet the elder."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6231, Sahih Muslim 2160 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Islamic social etiquette prescribes a harmonious hierarchy for initiating greetings, teaching humility and respect.

### Hadith #0654
* **Topic / Chapter:** Greet Children to Cultivate Affection / التَّسْلِيمُ عَلَى الصِّبْيَانِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ: **«أَنَّهُ مَرَّ عَلَى صِبْيَانٍ فَسَلَّمَ عَلَيْهِمْ، وَقَالَ: كَانَ النَّبِيُّ ﷺ يَفْعَلُهُ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Anas ibn Malik (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > Anas ibn Malik passed by some young boys and greeted them with Salam, saying: "The Prophet ﷺ used to do that."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6247, Sahih Muslim 2168 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Greeting children with Salam demonstrates prophetic humility, instills Islamic identity, and teaches young minds proper etiquette.

### Hadith #0655
* **Topic / Chapter:** Sins Falling Away During Handshakes / تَسَاقُطُ الذُّنُوبِ بِالْمُصَافَحَةِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنِ الْبَرَاءِ بْنِ عَازِبٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«مَا مِنْ مُسْلِمَيْنِ يَلْتَقِيَانِ فَيَتَصَافَحَانِ إِلَّا غُفِرَ لَهُمَا قَبْلَ أَنْ يَفْتَرِقَا»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Al-Bara' ibn 'Azib (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "There are no two Muslims who meet and shake hands except that their sins are forgiven before they part."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sunan Abi Dawud 5212, Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2727 (Sahih according to al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Physical handshakes coupled with warm smiles purify interpersonal relations and expiate minor sins between believers.

### Hadith #0656
* **Topic / Chapter:** Seeking Permission to Enter (Isti'dhan) Three Times / الِاسْتِئْذَانُ ثَلَاثٌ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي مُوسَى الْأَشْعَرِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«الِاسْتِئْذَانُ ثَلَاثٌ؛ فَإِنْ أُذِنَ لَكَ وَإِلَّا فَارْجِعْ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Musa al-Ash'ari (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Seeking permission to enter is to be done three times; if permission is granted to you, enter; otherwise, depart."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6245, Sahih Muslim 2153 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Respecting the privacy of households: knock or ring three times calmly, and leave gracefully without taking offense if no answer is given.

### Hadith #0657
* **Topic / Chapter:** Seeking Permission Protects Privacy of the Eyes / إِنَّمَا جُعِلَ الِاسْتِئْذَانُ مِنْ أَجْلِ الْبَصَرِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ سَهْلِ بْنِ سَعْدٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«إِنَّمَا جُعِلَ الِاسْتِئْذَانُ مِنْ أَجْلِ الْبَصَرِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Sahl ibn Sa'd (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Seeking permission to enter was instituted solely on account of protecting the sight (from seeing private household affairs)."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6241, Sahih Muslim 2156 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Stand to the side of doors when knocking rather than directly facing the opening to avoid catching sight of people in informal attire.

### Hadith #0658
* **Topic / Chapter:** Stating One's Clear Name When Knocking / كَرَاهَةُ أَنْ يَقُولَ: أَنَا
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ جَابِرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: أَتَيْتُ النَّبِيَّ ﷺ فِي دَيْنٍ كَانَ عَلَى أَبِي، فَدَقَقْتُ الْبَابَ، فَقَالَ: **«مَنْ ذَا؟»** فَقُلْتُ: أَنَا، فَقَالَ: **«أَنَا، أَنَا!»** كَأَنَّهُ كَرِهَهَا.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Jabir ibn 'Abdullah (رضي الله عنهما).
* **English Translation:**
  > Jabir reported: I came to the Prophet ﷺ regarding a debt my father owed. I knocked on the door, and he asked: "Who is that?" I said: "It is I." He repeated: "'It is I, it is I!' as if he disliked that response."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6250, Sahih Muslim 2155 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** When asked "Who is it?", one should state their clear identifying name (e.g. "It is Ahmad") rather than ambiguous words like "Me".

### Hadith #0659
* **Topic / Chapter:** Prohibition of Forcing Someone from Their Seat / نَهْيُ الرَّجُلِ أَنْ يُقِيمَ أَخَاهُ مِنْ مَجْلِسِهِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ: **«أَنَّهُ نَهَى أَنْ يُقِيمَ الرَّجُلُ أَخَاهُ مِنْ مَقْعَدِهِ ثُمَّ يَجْلِسَ فِيهِ، وَلَكِنْ تَفَسَّحُوا وَتَوَسَّعُوا»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn 'Umar (رضي الله عنهما).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Prophet ﷺ forbade that a man should make his brother stand up from his seat and then sit in it himself; rather, you should make space and accommodate one another.
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6269, Sahih Muslim 2177 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Islamic assemblies respect equal dignity; none should be displaced for high status, but people should adjust to welcome newcomers.

### Hadith #0660
* **Topic / Chapter:** Returning to One's Seat After a Brief Departure / إِذَا قَامَ أَحَدُكُمْ مِنْ مَجْلِسِهِ ثُمَّ رَجَعَ فَهُوَ أَحَقُّ بِهِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**

* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه).

  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** A person retains ownership of their seat when temporarily stepping away for water, Wudu, or an urgent errand.

### Hadith #0661
* **Topic / Chapter:** Fundamental Etiquette of Eating / آدَابُ الطَّعَامِ: سَمِّ اللَّهَ وَكُلْ بِيَمِينِكَ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ عُمَرَ بْنِ أَبِي سَلَمَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: كُنْتُ غُلَامًا فِي حَجْرِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﷺ، وَكَانَتْ يَدِي تَطِيشُ فِي الصَّحْفَةِ، فَقَالَ لِي رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«يَا غُلَامُ، سَمِّ اللَّهَ، وَكُلْ بِيَمِينِكَ، وَكُلْ مِمَّا يَلِيكَ»**، فَمَا زَالَتْ تِلْكَ طِعْمَتِي بَعْدُ.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'Umar ibn Abi Salamah (رضي الله عنهما).
* **English Translation:**
  > 'Umar ibn Abi Salamah reported: I was a young boy in the care of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, and my hand used to wander around the dish. The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said to me: "O young boy, say Bismillah (In the Name of Allah), eat with your right hand, and eat from what is directly in front of you." That continued to be my manner of eating ever since.
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 5376, Sahih Muslim 2022 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** The three golden rules of dining etiquette: invoking Allah's Name, using the right hand exclusively, and taking food from the area nearest to oneself.

### Hadith #0662
* **Topic / Chapter:** Strict Prohibition of Eating and Drinking with the Left Hand / النَّهْيُ عَنِ الْأَكْلِ وَالشُّرْبِ بِالشِّمَالِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: **«لَا يَأْكُلَنَّ أَحَدُكُمْ بِشِمَالِهِ، وَلَا يَشْرَبَنَّ بِهَا؛ فَإِنَّ الشَّيْطَانَ يَأْكُلُ بِشِمَالِهِ، وَيَشْرَبُ بِهَا»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn 'Umar (رضي الله عنهما).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "None of you should eat with his left hand nor drink with it; for indeed, Satan eats with his left hand and drinks with it."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 2020 (Kitab al-Ashribah, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** The right hand is reserved for noble activities (eating, drinking, giving gifts), while the left is used for cleaning impurities.

### Hadith #0663
* **Topic / Chapter:** Cleaning the Fallen Morsel of Food / إِمَاطَةُ الْأَذَى عَنِ اللُّقْمَةِ السَّاقِطَةِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ جَابِرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«إِذَا سَقَطَتْ لُقْمَةُ أَحَدِكُمْ فَلْيَأْخُذْهَا، فَلْيُمِطْ مَا كَانَ بِهَا مِنْ أَذًى وَلْيَأْكُلْهَا، وَلَا يَدَعْهَا لِلشَّيْطَانِ، وَلَا يَمْسَحْ يَدَهُ بِالْمِنْدِيلِ حَتَّى يَلْعَقَ أَصَابِعَهُ؛ فَإِنَّهُ لَا يَدْرِي فِي أَيِّ طَعَامِهِ الْبَرَكَةُ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Jabir ibn 'Abdullah (رضي الله عنهما).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "When a morsel falls from one of you, let him pick it up, remove any dirt from it, and eat it, and not leave it for Satan. And let him not wipe his hand with a napkin until he licks his fingers; for he does not know in which portion of his food the barakah (blessing) lies."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 2033 (Kitab al-Ashribah, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Respecting food, avoiding food waste out of arrogance, and seeking the divine barakah hidden within every provision.

### Hadith #0664
* **Topic / Chapter:** Never Criticizing Food / مَا عَابَ النَّبِيُّ ﷺ طَعَامًا قَطُّ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: **«مَا عَابَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ طَعَامًا قَطُّ؛ كَانَ إِذَا اشْتَهَاهُ أَكَلَهُ، وَإِنْ كَرِهَهُ تَرَكَهُ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > Abu Hurairah reported: "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ never criticized any food whatsoever. If he desired it, he ate it; and if he disliked it, he simply left it."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 5409, Sahih Muslim 2064 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Never belittle food prepared by others; eat with gratitude or politely refrain without humiliating the cook.

### Hadith #0665
* **Topic / Chapter:** Drinking Water in Three Pauses Outside the Cup / الشُّرْبُ فِي ثَلَاثَةِ أَنْفَاسٍ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ: **«أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ كَانَ يَتَنَفَّسُ فِي الشَّرَابِ ثَلَاثًا، وَيَقُولُ: إِنَّهُ أَرْوَى وَأَبْرَأُ وَأَمْرَأُ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Anas ibn Malik (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > Anas ibn Malik reported that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ used to breathe three times (outside the vessel) while drinking, and he said: "Indeed, it is more thirst-quenching, healthier, and more wholesome."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 5631, Sahih Muslim 2028 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Drinking water in measured, calm sips with pauses away from the cup promotes optimal hydration and prevents respiratory choking.

### Hadith #0667
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Server of People is the Last to Drink / سَاقِي الْقَوْمِ آخِرُهُمْ شُرْبًا
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي قَتَادَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ: **«إِنَّ سَاقِيَ الْقَوْمِ آخِرُهُمْ شُرْبًا»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Qatadah (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Prophet ﷺ said: "Indeed, the one who serves drinks to the people should be the last of them to drink."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Jami' at-Tirmidhi 1894, Sahih Muslim 681 (Sahih according to at-Tirmidhi and al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** True leadership is servant leadership; a host ensures all guests are fully satisfied before attending to his own thirst.

### Hadith #0668

* **Topic / Chapter:** Preserving Modesty and Dignity in Attire / تَحْسِينُ الْهَيْئَةِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ جَابِرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: أَتَانَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ فَرَأَى رَجُلًا شَعِثًا قَدْ تَفَرَّقَ شَعْرُهُ، فَقَالَ: **«أَمَا كَانَ يَجِدُ هَذَا مَا يُسَكِّنُ بِهِ شَعْرَهُ؟»**، وَرَأَى رَجُلًا آخَرَ وَعَلَيْهِ ثِيَابٌ وَسِخَةٌ، فَقَالَ: **«أَمَا كَانَ هَذَا يَجِدُ مَاءً يَغْسِلُ بِهِ ثَوْبَهُ؟»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Jabir ibn 'Abdullah (رضي الله عنهما).
* **English Translation:**
  > Jabir reported: The Messenger of Allah ﷺ visited us and saw a disheveled man whose hair was untidy. He said: "Could this man not find something to tidy his hair with?" And he saw another man wearing filthy clothes, and said: "Could this man not find water to wash his clothes with?"
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sunan Abi Dawud 4062, Sunan an-Nasa'i 5236 (Sahih according to al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Islam promotes clean garments, groomed hair, and neat appearances as reflections of aesthetic beauty and self-respect.

### Hadith #0701
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Prophet's Mission to Perfect Noble Moral Character / إِنَّمَا بُعِثْتُ لِأُتَمِّمَ صَالِحَ الْأَخْلَاقِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«إِنَّمَا بُعِثْتُ لِأُتَمِّمَ صَالِحَ الْأَخْلَاقِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "I was only sent to perfect the highest standards of noble moral character (Makarim al-Akhlaq)."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Al-Adab al-Mufrad 273, Musnad Ahmad 8952 (Sahih according to al-Albani in As-Silsilah as-Sahihah 45, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** The ultimate culmination of the Islamic message is the perfection of human character, integrity, compassion, and refined manners.
