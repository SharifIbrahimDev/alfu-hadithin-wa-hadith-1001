### Hadith #0952 | الحديث رقم: 0952

#### 🇸🇦 القسم العربي (Arabic Text & Takhrij)
<div dir="rtl" align="right">

##### **[الباب: كِتَابُ الْفِتَنِ وَأَشْرَاطِ السَّاعَةِ — الْمُبَادَرَةُ بِالْأَعْمَالِ الصَّالِحَةِ]**

> **نَصُّ الْحَدِيثِ بِالتَّشْكِيلِ:**  
> عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: «بَادِرُوا بِالْأَعْمَالِ فِتَنًا كَقِطَعِ اللَّيْلِ الْمُظْلِمِ، يُصْبِحُ الرَّجُلُ مُؤْمِنًا وَيُمْسِي كَافِرًا، أَوْ يُمْسِي مُؤْمِنًا وَيُصْبِحُ كَافِرًا، يَبِيعُ دِينَهُ بِعَرَضٍ مِنَ الدُّنْيَا».

* **التَّخْرِيجُ:** Sahih Muslim (118) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (صحيح).
* **الْحُكْمُ:** Sahih Muslim (118) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (صحيح).

</div>

#### 🇬🇧 English Translation & Commentary
##### **[Topic: Book of Tribulations & Signs of the Hour — Hastening to Good Deeds Before Tribulations Dark as Night]**

> **English Translation:**  
> *"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Hasten to perform righteous deeds before tribulations appear like patches of a pitch-dark night: a man will wake up as a believer in the morning and become a disbeliever by evening, or be a believer in the evening and become a disbeliever by morning, selling his religion for a fleeting portion of worldly gain."*

* **Companion Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (may Allah be pleased with him)
* **Canonical Reference:** Sahih Muslim (118) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (صحيح).
* **Scholarly Grading:** **Sahih Muslim (118) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (صحيح)**
* **Key Lessons & Takeaways:** An urgent command to fortify one's soul with consistent righteous deeds before confusing spiritual and social crises compromise faith.

### Hadith #0953 | الحديث رقم: 0953

#### 🇸🇦 القسم العربي (Arabic Text & Takhrij)
<div dir="rtl" align="right">

##### **[الباب: كِتَابُ الْفِتَنِ وَأَشْرَاطِ السَّاعَةِ — مَوَاقِعُ الْفِتَنِ]**

> **نَصُّ الْحَدِيثِ بِالتَّشْكِيلِ:**  
> عَنْ أُسَامَةَ بْنِ زَيْدٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: أَشْرَفَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَلَى أُطُمٍ مِنْ آطَامِ الْمَدِينَةِ، فَقَالَ: «هَلْ تَرَوْنَ مَا أَرَى؟ إِنِّي لَأَرَى مَوَاقِعَ الْفِتَنِ خِلَالَ بُيُوتِكُمْ كَمَوَاقِعِ الْقَطْرِ».

* **التَّخْرِيجُ:** Sahih al-Bukhari (1878), Sahih Muslim (2885) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).
* **الْحُكْمُ:** Sahih al-Bukhari (1878), Sahih Muslim (2885) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).

</div>

#### 🇬🇧 English Translation & Commentary
##### **[Topic: Book of Tribulations & Signs of the Hour — Tribulations Falling Like Raindrops]**

> **English Translation:**  
> *"The Prophet ﷺ stood atop one of the elevated forts of Madinah and said: "Do you see what I see? Indeed, I see the locations of tribulations falling amidst your houses like the downpour of raindrops!"*

* **Companion Narrator:** Narrated by Usamah ibn Zayd (may Allah be pleased with them both)
* **Canonical Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari (1878), Sahih Muslim (2885) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).
* **Scholarly Grading:** **Sahih al-Bukhari (1878), Sahih Muslim (2885) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه)**
* **Key Lessons & Takeaways:** Prophetic foresight warning future generations of pervasive trials and civil strife that would test the integrity of homes.

### Hadith #0955 | الحديث رقم: 0955

#### 🇸🇦 القسم العربي (Arabic Text & Takhrij)
<div dir="rtl" align="right">

##### **[الباب: كِتَابُ الْفِتَنِ وَأَشْرَاطِ السَّاعَةِ — عَرْضُ الْفِتَنِ عَلَى الْقُلُوبِ]**

> **نَصُّ الْحَدِيثِ بِالتَّشْكِيلِ:**  
> عَنْ حُذَيْفَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ: «تُعْرَضُ الْفِتَنُ عَلَى الْقُلُوبِ كَالْحَصِيرِ عُودًا عُودًا، فَأَيُّ قَلْبٍ أُشْرِبَهَا نُكِتَ فِيهِ نُكْتَةٌ سَوْدَاءُ، وَأَيُّ قَلْبٍ أَنْكَرَهَا نُكِتَ فِيهِ نُكْتَةٌ بَيْضَاءُ، حَتَّى تَصِيرَ عَلَى قَلْبَيْنِ: عَلَى أَبْيَضَ مِثْلِ الصَّفَا فَلَا تَضُرُّهُ فِتْنَةٌ مَا دَامَتِ السَّمَاوَاتُ وَالْأَرْضُ، وَالْآخَرِ أَسْوَدَ مُرْبَادًّا كَالْكُوزِ مُجَخِّيًا، لَا يَعْرِفُ مَعْرُوفًا وَلَا يُنْكِرُ مُنْكَرًا، إِلَّا مَا أُشْرِبَ مِنْ هَوَاهُ».

* **التَّخْرِيجُ:** Sahih Muslim (144) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (صحيح).
* **الْحُكْمُ:** Sahih Muslim (144) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (صحيح).

</div>

#### 🇬🇧 English Translation & Commentary
##### **[Topic: Book of Tribulations & Signs of the Hour — Tribulations Presented to Hearts Like Woven Reeds]**

> **English Translation:**  
> *"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Trials and tribulations will be presented to hearts repeatedly like the reeds of a woven mat, strand by strand. Any heart that absorbs them will have a black spot marked upon it, and any heart that rejects them will have a white spot marked upon it, until hearts become of two types: a white heart like smooth stone that will never be harmed by any tribulation as long as the heavens and earth endure; and the other a black, dusty heart like an upturned vessel, neither recognizing good nor repudiating evil, except what aligns with its own vain desires."*

* **Companion Narrator:** Narrated by Hudhayfah ibn al-Yaman (may Allah be pleased with him)
* **Canonical Reference:** Sahih Muslim (144) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (صحيح).
* **Scholarly Grading:** **Sahih Muslim (144) — Maktaba Shamela. Grading: Sahih (صحيح)**
* **Key Lessons & Takeaways:** Exposing the soul to repeated sins and corrupt ideologies slowly desensitizes the heart until it completely loses the ability to discern truth from falsehood.

### Hadith #0956 | الحديث رقم: 0956

#### 🇸🇦 القسم العربي (Arabic Text & Takhrij)
<div dir="rtl" align="right">

##### **[الباب: كِتَابُ الْفِتَنِ وَأَشْرَاطِ السَّاعَةِ — بَابُ الْفِتْنَةِ]**

> **نَصُّ الْحَدِيثِ بِالتَّشْكِيلِ:**  
> عَنْ حُذَيْفَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: كُنَّا جُلُوسًا عِنْدَ عُمَرَ فَقَالَ: أَيُّكُمْ يَحْفَظُ قَوْلَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فِي الْفِتْنَةِ؟... فَقَالَ عُمَرُ: لَيْسَ هَذَا أُرِيدُ، وَلَكِنِ الْفِتْنَةُ الَّتِي تَمُوجُ كَمَوْجِ الْبَحْرِ. قَالَ: لَيْسَ عَلَيْكَ مِنْهَا بَأْسٌ يَا أَمِيرَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ، إِنَّ بَيْنَكَ وَبَيْنَهَا بَابًا مُغْلَقًا. قَالَ عُمَرُ: أَيُكْسَرُ الْبَابُ أَمْ يُفْتَحُ؟ قَالَ: لَا، بَلْ يُكْسَرُ. قَالَ عُمَرُ: إِذًا لَا يُغْلَقَ أَبَدًا... وَالْبَابُ عُمَرُ.

* **التَّخْرِيجُ:** Sahih al-Bukhari (1435), Sahih Muslim (144) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).
* **الْحُكْمُ:** Sahih al-Bukhari (1435), Sahih Muslim (144) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).

</div>

#### 🇬🇧 English Translation & Commentary
##### **[Topic: Book of Tribulations & Signs of the Hour — 'Umar as the Protective Door Against Civil Strife]**

> **English Translation:**  
> *"Umar asked: "Who among you remembers what the Prophet ﷺ said about the tribulation that will surge like oceanic waves?" Hudhayfah replied: "You need not fear it, O Commander of the Faithful; between you and it is a locked door." 'Umar asked: "Will the door be opened or broken?" Hudhayfah said: "No, it will be broken violently (by your martyrdom)." 'Umar said: "Then it will never be closed again!" ... And the door was 'Umar himself."*

* **Companion Narrator:** Narrated by Hudhayfah (may Allah be pleased with him)
* **Canonical Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari (1435), Sahih Muslim (144) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).
* **Scholarly Grading:** **Sahih al-Bukhari (1435), Sahih Muslim (144) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه)**
* **Key Lessons & Takeaways:** The justice and firmness of 'Umar held the gates against internal dissension; his martyrdom opened the floodgates of historical civil tribulations.

##### **[الباب: كِتَابُ الْفِتَنِ وَأَشْرَاطِ السَّاعَةِ — كَلِمَتَانِ حَبِيبَتَانِ إِلَى الرَّحْمَنِ]**

> **نَصُّ الْحَدِيثِ بِالتَّشْكِيلِ:**  
> عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «كَلِمَتَانِ خَفِيفَتَانِ عَلَى اللِّسَانِ، ثَقِيلَتَانِ فِي الْمِيزَانِ، حَبِيبَتَانِ إِلَى الرَّحْمَنِ: سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ، سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ الْعَظِيمِ».

* **التَّخْرِيجُ:** Sahih al-Bukhari (7563 — the final Hadith of Sahih al-Bukhari), Sahih Muslim (2694) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).
* **الْحُكْمُ:** Sahih al-Bukhari (7563 — the final Hadith of Sahih al-Bukhari), Sahih Muslim (2694) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).

#### 🇬🇧 English Translation & Commentary
##### **[Topic: Book of Tribulations & Signs of the Hour — The Grand Culmination: Two Beloved Words Heavy in the Scale]**

> **English Translation:**  
> *"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Two phrases are light upon the tongue, heavy in the divine Balance (Al-Mizan), and beloved to the Most Merciful (Ar-Rahman): *'Subhan Allahi wa bi-hamdihi'* (Glory be to Allah and His is the praise), *'Subhan Allahil-'Azim'* (Glory be to Allah the Supreme)."*

* **Companion Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (may Allah be pleased with him)
* **Canonical Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari (7563 — the final Hadith of Sahih al-Bukhari), Sahih Muslim (2694) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه).
* **Scholarly Grading:** **Sahih al-Bukhari (7563 — the final Hadith of Sahih al-Bukhari), Sahih Muslim (2694) — Muttafaqun 'Alayh (متفق عليه)**
* **Key Lessons & Takeaways:** Concluding our compilation of 1001 authentic Prophetic traditions with the timeless glorification and praise of Allah Almighty—the supreme key to filling the Scales of Eternity with boundless light and divine love.
