# 15. كِتَابُ الْحِلْمِ وَحُسْنِ الْخُلُقِ
# Chapter 15: The Book of Forbearance & Moral Character

---

### Hadith #0702
* **Topic / Chapter:** Good Character is the Heaviest Deed on the Scale / أَثْقَلُ شَيْءٍ فِي الْمِيزَانِ حُسْنُ الْخُلُقِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي الدَّرْدَاءِ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ: **«مَا مِنْ شَيْءٍ أَثْقَلُ فِي مِيزَانِ الْمُؤْمِنِ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ مِنْ حُسْنِ الْخُلُقِ، وَإِنَّ اللَّهَ لَيُبْغِضُ الْفَاحِشَ الْبَذِيءَ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu al-Darda' (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Prophet ﷺ said: "There is nothing heavier on the Scale of the believer on the Day of Resurrection than good moral character; and indeed, Allah detests the vulgar, foul-mouthed person."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2002, Sunan Abi Dawud 4799 (Sahih according to at-Tirmidhi and al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Noble character, forbearance, and courteous speech outweigh countless voluntary rituals on the ultimate Day of Judgment.

### Hadith #0703
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Closest to the Prophet ﷺ on the Day of Judgment / أَقْرَبُكُمْ مِنِّي مَجْلِسًا يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ أَحَاسِنُكُمْ أَخْلَاقًا
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ جَابِرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: **«إِنَّ مِنْ أَحَبِّكُمْ إِلَيَّ وَأَقْرَبِكُمْ مِنِّي مَجْلِسًا يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ أَحَاسِنَكُمْ أَخْلَاقًا، وَإِنَّ أَبْغَضَكُمْ إِلَيَّ وَأَبْعَدَكُمْ مِنِّي مَجْلِسًا يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ الثَّرْثَارُونَ وَالْمُتَشَدِّقُونَ وَالْمُتَفَيْهِقُونَ»**، قَالُوا: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، قَدْ عَلِمْنَا الثَّرْثَارُونَ وَالْمُتَشَدِّقُونَ، فَمَا الْمُتَفَيْهِقُونَ؟ قَالَ: **«الْمُتَكَبِّرُونَ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Jabir ibn 'Abdullah (رضي الله عنهما).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Indeed, the most beloved of you to me and the closest of you to me in sitting on the Day of Resurrection are those of you with the best character. And indeed, the most detested of you to me and the farthest of you from me on the Day of Resurrection are the boastful prattlers (Thartharun), the pompous blowhards (Mutashaddiqun), and the Mutafayhiqun." They asked: "O Messenger of Allah, we know the prattlers and the pompous, but who are the Mutafayhiqun?" He replied: "The arrogant ones."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2018 (Sahih according to al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Eternal proximity to the Prophet ﷺ in Paradise is reserved for the humble, refined, and gentle, while pompous arrogance pushes the soul far away.

### Hadith #0704
* **Topic / Chapter:** Reaching the Rank of Perpetual Fasting and Night Prayer Through Character / دَرَجَةُ الصَّائِمِ الْقَائِمِ بِحُسْنِ الْخُلُقِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهَا قَالَتْ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ يَقُولُ: **«إِنَّ الْمُؤْمِنَ لَيُدْرِكُ بِحُسْنِ خُلُقِهِ دَرَجَةَ الصَّائِمِ الْقَائِمِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'A'ishah (رضي الله عنها).
* **English Translation:**
  > 'A'ishah narrated: I heard the Messenger of Allah ﷺ say: "Indeed, by virtue of his noble character, a believer surely attains the spiritual rank of the one who constantly fasts and spends the nights in prayer."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sunan Abi Dawud 4798 (Sahih according to al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Enduring interpersonal difficulties with forbearance and grace elevates a person to the heavenly heights achieved by ascetic worshipers.

### Hadith #0705
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Real Strong Man Controls Himself When Angry / لَيْسَ الشَّدِيدُ بِالصُّرَعَةِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: **«لَيْسَ الشَّدِيدُ بِالصُّرَعَةِ، إِنَّمَا الشَّدِيدُ الَّذِي يَمْلِكُ نَفْسَهُ عِنْدَ الْغَضَبِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "The strong person is not the one who overpowers rivals in wrestling; rather, the truly strong person is the one who controls himself in moments of anger."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6114, Sahih Muslim 2609 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** True heroism is psychological and spiritual mastery over wrath, impulses, and retaliatory pride.

### Hadith #0706
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Two Beloved Qualities: Forbearance and Deliberation / خَصْلَتَانِ يُحِبُّهُمَا اللَّهُ: الْحِلْمُ وَالْأَنَاةُ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ لِأَشَجِّ عَبْدِ الْقَيْسِ: **«إِنَّ فِيكَ خَصْلَتَيْنِ يُحِبُّهُمَا اللَّهُ: الْحِلْمُ، وَالْأَنَاةُ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn 'Abbas (رضي الله عنهما).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said to Ashajj 'Abd al-Qays: "Indeed, you possess two distinct qualities which Allah loves: forbearance (Al-Hilm) and calm deliberation (Al-Anah)."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 17 (Kitab al-Iman, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Forbearance in swallowing provocation and patience in avoiding hasty judgments represent two of the most beloved character traits to Allah.

### Hadith #0707
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Master Advice: "Do Not Become Angry" / لَا تَغْضَبْ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ: أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ جَاءَهُ رَجُلٌ فَقَالَ: أَوْصِنِي، قَالَ: **«لَا تَغْضَبْ»**، فَرَدَّدَ مِرَارًا، قَالَ: **«لَا تَغْضَبْ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > A man came to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and said: "Advise me." The Prophet ﷺ said: "Do not become angry." The man repeated his request several times, and each time the Prophet ﷺ said: "Do not become angry."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6116 (Kitab al-Adab, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Restraining anger preserves physical health, mental sanity, familial relationships, and spiritual salvation from all avenues of ruin.

### Hadith #0708
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Celestial Reward for Suppressing Anger / كَظْمُ الْغَيْظِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ سَهْلِ بْنِ مُعَاذِ بْنِ أَنَسٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: **«مَنْ كَظَمَ غَيْظًا وَهُوَ قَادِرٌ عَلَى أَنْ يُنْفِذَهُ، دَعَاهُ اللَّهُ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ عَلَى رُءُوسِ الْخَلَائِقِ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ حَتَّى يُخَيِّرَهُ مِنَ الْحُورِ الْعِينِ مَا شَاءَ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Mu'adh ibn Anas (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Whoever suppresses his rage while possessing the power to execute it, Allah, Mighty and Majestic, will summon him before the heads of all creation on the Day of Resurrection and allow him to choose whichever of the wide-eyed maidens of Paradise (Al-Hoor al-'Iyn) he desires."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sunan Abi Dawud 4777, Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2021 (Hasan according to at-Tirmidhi and al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Hasan (حسن).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Choosing gracious restraint over revenge when holding absolute power to strike back earns the highest public honors before all assembled creation on Judgment Day.

### Hadith #0709
* **Topic / Chapter:** Practical De-escalation Techniques for Anger / تَسْكِينُ الْغَضَبِ بِالْقُعُودِ وَالِاضْطِجَاعِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي ذَرٍّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ لَنَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«إِذَا غَضِبَ أَحَدُكُمْ وَهُوَ قَائِمٌ فَلْيَجْلِسْ، فَإِنْ ذَهَبَ عَنْهُ الْغَضَبُ وَإِلَّا فَلْيَضْطَجِعْ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Dharr al-Ghifari (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said to us: "When one of you becomes angry while standing, let him sit down. If his anger departs from him, well and good; otherwise, let him lie down."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sunan Abi Dawud 4782 (Sahih according to al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Changing physical posture from standing to sitting and lying down lowers blood pressure, reduces aggressive impulse, and prevents rash physical retaliation.

### Hadith #0710
* **Topic / Chapter:** Pardoning Others Only Increases Honor / مَا زَادَ اللَّهُ عَبْدًا بِعَفْوٍ إِلَّا عِزًّا
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: **«مَا نَقَصَتْ صَدَقَةٌ مِنْ مَالٍ، وَمَا زَادَ اللَّهُ عَبْدًا بِعَفْوٍ إِلَّا عِزًّا، وَمَا تَوَاضَعَ أَحَدٌ لِلَّهِ إِلَّا رَفَعَهُ اللَّهُ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Charity never diminishes wealth; and Allah never increases a servant who pardons except in honor; and no one humbles himself for the sake of Allah except that Allah elevates him."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 2588 (Kitab al-Birr was-Silah wal-Adab, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Forgiving wrongdoers is not weakness; Allah crowns the forgiving soul with enduring prestige, respect, and elevation in both worlds.

### Hadith #0711
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Prophet Never Took Personal Revenge / مَا انْتَقَمَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ لِنَفْسِهِ قَطُّ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهَا قَالَتْ: **«مَا خُيِّرَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ بَيْنَ أَمْرَيْنِ إِلَّا أَخَذَ أَيْسَرَهُمَا، مَا لَمْ يَكُنْ إِثْمًا، فَإِنْ كَانَ إِثْمًا كَانَ أَبْعَدَ النَّاسِ مِنْهُ، وَمَا انْتَقَمَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ لِنَفْسِهِ، إِلَّا أَنْ تُنْتَهَكَ حُرْمَةُ اللَّهِ فَيَنْتَقِمَ لِلَّهِ بِهَا»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'A'ishah (رضي الله عنها).
* **English Translation:**
  > 'A'ishah narrated: "Never was the Messenger of Allah ﷺ given the choice between two matters except that he chose the easier of the two, as long as it was not a sin; if it was a sin, he was the farthest of all people from it. And never did the Messenger of Allah ﷺ take revenge for himself in anything, unless the sacred boundaries of Allah were violated, whereupon he would avenge for the sake of Allah."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6126, Sahih Muslim 2327 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Complete transcendence over personal grievances: adopting ease, overlooking insults, and reserving indignation solely for defending divine laws.

### Hadith #0712
* **Topic / Chapter:** Forbearing the Harshness of the Bedouin / حِلْمُ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ مَعَ جَفَاءِ الْأَعْرَابِيِّ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: كُنْتُ أَمْشِي مَعَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﷺ وَعَلَيْهِ بُرْدٌ نَجْرَانِيٌّ غَلِيظُ الْحَاشِيَةِ، فَأَدْرَكَهُ أَعْرَابِيٌّ فَجَبَذَهُ بِرِدَائِهِ جَبْذَةً شَدِيدَةً، حَتَّى نَظَرْتُ إِلَى صَفْحَةِ عَاتِقِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَدْ أَثَّرَتْ بِهَا حَاشِيَةُ الْبُرْدِ مِنْ شِدَّةِ جَبْذَتِهِ، ثُمَّ قَالَ: يَا مُحَمَّدُ، مُرْ لِي مِنْ مَالِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي عِنْدَكَ، فَالْتَفَتَ إِلَيْهِ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ فَضَحِكَ، ثُمَّ أَمَرَ لَهُ بِعَطَاءٍ.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Anas ibn Malik (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > Anas ibn Malik reported: I was walking with the Messenger of Allah ﷺ who was wearing a thick-bordered Najrani cloak. A bedouin caught up with him and violently jerked his cloak so hard that I looked at the side of the Prophet's neck and saw the border of the cloak had left a red mark. The bedouin demanded: "O Muhammad! Order that I be given from Allah's wealth which you possess!" The Messenger of Allah ﷺ turned to him, smiled, and then ordered that a gift of provision be given to him.
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 3149, Sahih Muslim 1057 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Unmatched magnanimity: greeting physical rudeness and coarse demands with a warm smile and spontaneous generosity.

### Hadith #0713
* **Topic / Chapter:** Truthfulness Leads to Righteousness and Paradise / الصِّدْقُ يَهْدِي إِلَى الْبِرِّ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ مَسْعُودٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ: **«إِنَّ الصِّدْقَ يَهْدِي إِلَى الْبِرِّ، وَإِنَّ الْبِرَّ يَهْدِي إِلَى الْجَنَّةِ، وَإِنَّ الرَّجُلَ لَيَصْدُقُ حَتَّى يَكُونَ صِدِّيقًا، وَإِنَّ الْكَذِبَ يَهْدِي إِلَى الْفُجُورِ، وَإِنَّ الْفُجُورَ يَهْدِي إِلَى النَّارِ، وَإِنَّ الرَّجُلَ لَيَكْذِبُ حَتَّى يُكْتَبَ عِنْدَ اللَّهِ كَذَّابًا»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Prophet ﷺ said: "Truthfulness leads to righteousness, and righteousness leads to Paradise; and a man continues to speak the truth until he is recorded with Allah as a champion of truth (Siddiq). And falsehood leads to wickedness, and wickedness leads to Hellfire; and a man continues to tell lies until he is recorded with Allah as an inveterate liar."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6094, Sahih Muslim 2607 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Truthfulness in words and deeds shapes righteous destiny culminating in Paradise, while casual dishonesty corrupts character toward destruction.

### Hadith #0714
* **Topic / Chapter:** Four Core Signs of Hypocrisy in Moral Conduct / أَرْبَعُ خِلَالٍ مَنْ كُنَّ فِيهِ كَانَ مُنَافِقًا
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَمْرٍو رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا، أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ ﷺ قَالَ: **«أَرْبَعٌ مَنْ كُنَّ فِيهِ كَانَ مُنَافِقًا خَالِصًا، وَمَنْ كَانَتْ فِيهِ خَصْلَةٌ مِنْهُنَّ كَانَتْ فِيهِ خَصْلَةٌ مِنَ النِّفَاقِ حَتَّى يَدَعَهَا: إِذَا اؤْتُمِنَ خَانَ، وَإِذَا حَدَّثَ كَذَبَ، وَإِذَا عَاهَدَ غَدَرَ، وَإِذَا خَاصَمَ فَجَرَ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn 'Amr (رضي الله عنهما).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Prophet ﷺ said: "Four traits, whoever possesses them is a pure hypocrite, and whoever possesses one of them has a trait of hypocrisy until he abandons it: when he is entrusted, he betrays; when he speaks, he lies; when he makes a covenant, he acts treacherously; and when he disputes, he resorts to foulness and insolence."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 34, Sahih Muslim 58 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Hypocrisy is manifested in behavioral degradation: dishonesty, betrayal of trusts, breaking treaties, and obscene conduct during arguments.

### Hadith #0715
* **Topic / Chapter:** Softness and Accessibility Forbids Hellfire / كُلُّ هَيِّنٍ لَيِّنٍ سَهْلٍ قَرِيبٍ مِنَ النَّاسِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**

### Hadith #0732

### Hadith #0733

* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ: **«لَا تَكُونُوا إِمَّعَةً، تَقُولُونَ: إِنْ أَحْسَنَ النَّاسُ أَحْسَنَّا، وَإِنْ ظَلَمُوا ظَلَمْنَا، وَلَكِنْ وَطِّنُوا أَنْفُسَكُمْ: إِنْ أَحْسَنَ النَّاسُ أَنْ تُحْسِنُوا، وَإِنْ أَسَاءُوا فَلَا تَظْلِمُوا»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Hudhayfah ibn al-Yaman (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Prophet ﷺ said: "Do not be conformists without principles (Imma'ah), saying: 'If people do good, we will do good; and if they do wrong, we will do wrong.' Rather, condition yourselves: if people do good, do good; and if they do evil, do not commit injustice."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2007 (Hasan according to at-Tirmidhi and al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Hasan (حسن).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Moral integrity is autonomous and unshakeable; believers do not mirror the bad behavior of others, but stand firmly upon justice.

### Hadith #0743
* **Topic / Chapter:** Moderation in Love and Aversion / الْقَصْدُ فِي الْحُبِّ وَالْبُغْضِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، رَفَعَهُ، قَالَ: **«أَحْبِبْ حَبِيبَكَ هَوْنًا مَا عَسَى أَنْ يَكُونَ بَغِيضَكَ يَوْمًا مَا، وَأَبْغِضْ بَغِيضَكَ هَوْنًا مَا عَسَى أَنْ يَكُونَ حَبِيبَكَ يَوْمًا مَا»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Prophet ﷺ said: "Love your friend with moderation, for he may become your adversary one day; and dislike your adversary with moderation, for he may become your friend one day."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Jami' at-Tirmidhi 1997 (Sahih according to al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Emotional balance in relationships: avoiding excessive disclosure or blind attachment, and avoiding scorched-earth enmity.

### Hadith #0744

  * **Grading:** Hasan (حسن).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** The believer chooses to overlook petty tricks with aristocratic grace, preferring noble generosity over malicious cunning.

### Hadith #0746
* **Topic / Chapter:** Giving Advice to the Young with Tender Compassion / وَصِيَّةُ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ لِابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: كُنْتُ خَلْفَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﷺ يَوْمًا فَقَالَ: **«يَا غُلَامُ، إِنِّي أُعَلِّمُكَ كَلِمَاتٍ: احْفَظِ اللَّهَ يَحْفَظْكَ، احْفَظِ اللَّهَ تَجِدْهُ تُجَاهَكَ، إِذَا سَأَلْتَ فَاسْأَلِ اللَّهَ، وَإِذَا اسْتَعَنْتَ فَاسْتَعِنْ بِاللَّهِ، وَاعْلَمْ أَنَّ الْأُمَّةَ لَوِ اجْتَمَعَتْ عَلَى أَنْ يَنْفَعُوكَ بِشَيْءٍ لَمْ يَنْفَعُوكَ إِلَّا بِشَيْءٍ قَدْ كَتَبَهُ اللَّهُ لَكَ، وَلَوِ اجْتَمَعُوا عَلَى أَنْ يَضُرُّوكَ بِشَيْءٍ لَمْ يَضُرُّوكَ إِلَّا بِشَيْءٍ قَدْ كَتَبَهُ اللَّهُ عَلَيْكَ، رُفِعَتِ الْأَقْلَامُ وَجَفَّتِ الصُّحُفُ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by 'Abdullah ibn 'Abbas (رضي الله عنهما).
* **English Translation:**
  > Ibn 'Abbas reported: One day I was riding behind the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and he said: "O young boy, I shall teach you some words: Be mindful of Allah and He will protect you. Be mindful of Allah and you will find Him before you. If you ask, ask of Allah; and if you seek help, seek help from Allah. And know that if the entire nation were to gather together to benefit you with something, they could not benefit you except with what Allah has already written for you; and if they were to gather together to harm you with something, they could not harm you except with what Allah has already written against you. The pens have been lifted and the pages have dried."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2516 (Sahih according to at-Tirmidhi and al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Mentoring youth with profound theological foundations: unwavering trust in Allah, self-reliance, and freedom from fear of creation.

### Hadith #0748
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Superiority of Forbearing Injustice / الصَّبْرُ عَلَى الْمَظْلَمَةِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ: أَنَّ رَجُلًا شَتَمَ أَبَا بَكْرٍ وَالنَّبِيُّ ﷺ جَالِسٌ يَعْجَبُ وَيَتَبَسَّمُ، فَلَمَّا أَكْثَرَ رَدَّ عَلَيْهِ أَبُو بَكْرٍ بَعْضَ قَوْلِهِ، فَغَضِبَ النَّبِيُّ ﷺ وَقَامَ، فَلَحِقَهُ أَبُو بَكْرٍ فَقَالَ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، كَانَ يَشْتُمُنِي وَأَنْتَ جَالِسٌ، فَلَمَّا رَدَدْتُ عَلَيْهِ قُمْتَ؟ فَقَالَ: **«إِنَّهُ كَانَ مَعَكَ مَلَكٌ يَرُدُّ عَنْكَ، فَلَمَّا رَدَدْتَ عَلَيْهِ وَقَعَ الشَّيْطَانُ، فَلَمْ أَكُنْ لِأَقْعُدَ مَعَ الشَّيْطَانِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه).
* **English Translation:**
  > A man verbally reviled Abu Bakr while the Prophet ﷺ was sitting, marveling and smiling. When the man exceeded limits, Abu Bakr responded to some of his remarks. Thereupon, the Prophet ﷺ became displeased and stood up. Abu Bakr followed him and asked: "O Messenger of Allah, he was insulting me while you sat smiling; but when I responded to him, you stood up?" The Prophet ﷺ said: "An angel was with you defending you and replying on your behalf; but when you responded yourself, Satan entered, and I was not going to sit with Satan!"
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sunan Abi Dawud 4896, Musnad Ahmad 9622 (Hasan/Sahih according to al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Hasan (حسن).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Remaining silent and patient under verbal abuse invites angelic defense; retaliating personally invites satanic escalation.

### Hadith #0749
* **Topic / Chapter:** Pure Sincerity in Brotherhood for Allah's Sake / الْمُتَحَابُّونَ فِي اللَّهِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ: **«أَنَّ رَجُلًا زَارَ أَخًا لَهُ فِي قَرْيَةٍ أُخْرَى، فَأَرْصَدَ اللَّهُ لَهُ عَلَى مَدْرَجَتِهِ مَلَكًا... فَقَالَ: إِنِّي أُحِبُّهُ فِي اللَّهِ، قَالَ: فَإِنِّي رَسُولُ اللَّهِ إِلَيْكَ بِأَنَّ اللَّهَ قَدْ أَحَبَّكَ كَمَا أَحْبَبْتَهُ فِيهِ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Hurairah (رضي الله عنه).

* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih Muslim 2567 (Kitab al-Birr was-Silah wal-Adab, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih (صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Loving others purely for Allah's sake without transactional motives earns the direct love of the Sovereign of the Heavens.

### Hadith #0750
* **Topic / Chapter:** Overcoming Personal Animosity to Save Lives / حِفْظُ حُرْمَةِ الدَّمِ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أُسَامَةَ بْنِ زَيْدٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: بَعَثَنَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ فِي سَرِيَّةٍ... فَلَحِقْتُ رَجُلًا فَقَالَ: لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، فَطَعَنْتُهُ فَقَتَلْتُهُ، فَوَقَعَ فِي نَفْسِي مِنْ ذَلِكَ، فَذَكَرْتُهُ لِلنَّبِيِّ ﷺ فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: **«أَقَالَ: لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَقَتَلْتَهُ؟»** قُلْتُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، إِنَّمَا قَالَهَا خَوْفًا مِنَ السِّلَاحِ، قَالَ: **«أَفَلَا شَقَقْتَ عَنْ قَلْبِهِ حَتَّى تَعْلَمَ أَقَالَهَا أَمْ لَا؟»**، فَمَا زَالَ يُكَرِّرُهَا عَلَيَّ حَتَّى تَمَنَّيْتُ أَنِّي أَسْلَمْتُ يَوْمَئِذٍ.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Usamah ibn Zayd (رضي الله عنهما).
* **English Translation:**
  > Usamah ibn Zayd reported: The Messenger of Allah ﷺ sent us on an expedition... I caught up with an enemy soldier and he said: 'La ilaha illallah', yet I pierced him with my spear and killed him. Unease settled in my heart, so I mentioned it to the Prophet ﷺ. The Messenger of Allah ﷺ asked: "Did he say 'La ilaha illallah' and you killed him?" I said: "O Messenger of Allah, he only said it to escape the sword!" He said: "Did you tear open his heart to know whether he was sincere or not?!" He kept repeating it to me until I wished I had not embraced Islam before that day!
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 4269, Sahih Muslim 96 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Sahih Muttafaqun 'Alayh (صحيح متفق عليه).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** Human beings are judged exclusively by outward declarations and actions; hidden intentions and inner hearts are known and judged solely by Allah.

### Hadith #0751
* **Topic / Chapter:** The Comprehensive Standard of Islamic Character / خَالِقِ النَّاسَ بِخُلُقٍ حَسَنٍ
* **Arabic Matn with Tashkeel:**
  > عَنْ أَبِي ذَرٍّ جُنْدَبِ بْنِ جُنَادَةَ، وَأَبِي عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ مُعَاذِ بْنِ جَبَلٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا، عَنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: **«اتَّقِ اللَّهَ حَيْثُمَا كُنْتَ، وَأَتْبِعِ السَّيِّئَةَ الْحَسَنَةَ تَمْحُهَا، وَخَالِقِ النَّاسَ بِخُلُقٍ حَسَنٍ»**.
* **Narrator:** Narrated by Abu Dharr al-Ghifari and Mu'adh ibn Jabal (رضي الله عنهما).
* **English Translation:**
  > The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Be mindful and fearful of Allah wherever you may be; follow up a bad deed with a good deed and it will wipe it out; and treat all people with noble moral character."
* **Reference & Grading:**
  * **Reference:** Jami' at-Tirmidhi 1987 (Hasan Sahih according to at-Tirmidhi and al-Albani, Shamela).
  * **Grading:** Hasan Sahih (حسن صحيح).
* **Key Benefit / Takeaway:** The ultimate triad of the believer's life: vertical piety with the Creator (Taqwa), continuous internal self-correction (Tawbah), and horizontal excellence with humanity (noble character).
