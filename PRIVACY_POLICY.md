# Privacy Policy for 1001 Authentic Hadith (ألف حديث وحديث)

**Last updated:** September 26, 2026

Thank you for using **1001 Authentic Hadith (ألف حديث وحديث)** ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe, transparent user experience.

---

### 1. Information Collection & Usage

#### a. Personal Information
- **We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).**
- You are not required to create an account, register, or provide an email address, name, phone number, or physical address to use the Application.

#### b. Device Data & Local Storage
- The Application operates completely **offline** for its core functionalities.
- All user preferences, including:
  - Reading progress and last-read Hadith
  - Bookmarked / Favorited Hadiths
  - Custom notes and highlights
  - Selected theme (Light, Dark, Sepia, Emerald) and font sizes
  - Daily notification reminder schedules
- Are stored **locally on your device** using secure on-device storage (`SharedPreferences` / SQLite).
- **No data is uploaded to external servers or cloud databases.**

#### c. Text-to-Speech (TTS) & Audio
- The audio recitation feature uses your device's built-in Text-to-Speech engine (Google TTS / Apple Speech Synthesis).
- Audio synthesis happens locally on your device. No voice data or recordings are captured, saved, or transmitted.

#### d. PDF Generation & Printing
- When you export Hadith chapters, thematic sections, or the complete 1001 Hadith book as PDF, the PDF is generated locally on your device.
- The generated PDF is saved only to your local storage or shared via standard device sharing intents initiated explicitly by you.

---

### 2. Device Permissions

The Application requests only the minimal permissions required for core features:

| Permission | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | To deliver optional daily Hadith reminder notifications at your chosen time. |
| `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` / `USE_EXACT_ALARM` | To ensure the daily Hadith reminder fires accurately at the user-scheduled time. |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | To reschedule your chosen daily reminder after your device reboots. |
| `WAKE_LOCK` / `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` | To ensure audio playback or notification scheduling completes without being abruptly killed by OS battery optimization. |

> **Note:** The Application does **NOT** access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, SMS, or phone state.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

- **No Third-Party Trackers:** The Application contains **NO** third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK) and **NO** advertising networks (such as AdMob or Unity Ads).
- **Zero Ads:** The Application is 100% free of commercial advertisements.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

The Application is suitable for users of all ages, including children. Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, we comply fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

---

### 5. Data Retention & Deletion

Since all user data (bookmarks, settings, notes) is stored strictly on your local device:
- You can clear your data at any time via the **Settings Screen > Reset Settings** inside the app.
- Uninstalling the Application permanently removes all locally stored data from your device.
